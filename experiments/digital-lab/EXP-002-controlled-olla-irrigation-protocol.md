# EXP-002 — Controlled Olla Irrigation Trial Protocol

**Date created:** 2026-09-18  
**Status:** Protocol prepared; no physical trial executed  
**Classification:** Not tested

## Objective
Evaluate whether porous clay-pot (olla) irrigation changes water use, root-zone moisture stability, and plant performance compared with matched irrigation controls.

## Falsifiable claim
Under the same soil, crop, weather, plot size, and plant-development conditions, olla irrigation will reduce supplied irrigation water for a predefined plant-performance target compared with the selected control method.

## Experimental design
Use a randomized block design with at least three independent replicates per treatment where space permits. Suggested treatments:

1. **Olla treatment:** buried porous vessel with measured capacity and delivery rate.
2. **Matched surface-irrigation treatment:** receives water according to a documented schedule.
3. **Reduced-water control:** receives a deliberately lower volume, only if plant welfare and trial ethics allow.
4. **Rain/no-irrigation reference:** optional and only where crop survival is not compromised.

Keep plot area, crop variety, plant age, planting density, soil preparation, mulch, sunlight exposure, and pest management as consistent as possible.

## Pre-registration requirements
Record before starting:

- Crop and cultivar
- Plot dimensions and number of plants
- Soil texture and baseline moisture
- Olla dimensions, material, wall condition, buried depth, and water capacity
- Olla delivery rate measured over a defined period
- Irrigation schedule and stopping rules
- Weather station or manual measurement method
- Primary endpoint and analysis method

## Measurements
Measure at consistent times:

- Water added to each plot, in litres
- Soil moisture at fixed depths and distances from the olla
- Soil temperature, if available
- Rainfall, air temperature, humidity, and wind exposure
- Plant survival, visible stress, height, leaf count, and phenology
- Final biomass or yield, using the same harvest method
- Olla refill frequency and leakage or breakage

Use calibrated measuring containers or flow meters. Record missing readings rather than replacing them silently.

## Suggested schedule
- **Baseline:** 3–7 days of soil and weather observations before treatment, where practical.
- **Treatment period:** minimum 30 days, or one clearly defined crop-development interval.
- **Observation frequency:** water volume daily; soil moisture at least once daily during the first week and thereafter at a fixed repeatable frequency; plant observations at least twice weekly.
- **Final assessment:** total water supplied, moisture variability, survival, growth, biomass/yield, and equipment condition.

## Primary outcomes
1. Litres of irrigation water supplied per surviving plant or per kilogram of harvested biomass.
2. Plant-performance measure selected before the trial.
3. Root-zone moisture mean and variability.

Secondary outcomes include labour time, refill frequency, vessel failure, and maintenance requirements.

## Controls against bias
- Randomize treatment placement within blocks.
- Label treatments using neutral identifiers during measurement review.
- Use the same measuring instruments and observation windows.
- Document deviations, missing data, pest events, and extreme weather.
- Do not remove unsuccessful plots without recording the reason.

## Analysis plan
Report treatment means, replicate-level values, spread measures, and confidence intervals where sample size supports them. Compare water supplied and plant-performance outcomes jointly; a water reduction accompanied by materially worse plant performance should not be described as a successful water-saving result. Avoid causal claims beyond the tested crop, soil, climate, and setup.

## Safety and ethics
Do not allow severe plant stress solely to complete the experiment. Stop or modify a treatment if plants show predefined serious distress, irrigation failure creates unsafe conditions, or contamination is observed. Handle water, tools, and soil amendments hygienically.

## Decision criteria
- **Supports the claim under tested conditions:** lower water supplied with comparable predefined plant performance and no unacceptable adverse effects.
- **Does not support the claim:** no water reduction, or water reduction accompanied by failure to meet the plant-performance target.
- **Inconclusive:** inadequate replication, missing data, uncontrolled confounding, or results too variable to interpret.

## Limitations
A single trial cannot establish universal olla performance. Results may depend on vessel porosity, soil texture, crop water demand, climate, installation depth, root distribution, and maintenance. Field data must be collected before making practical or commercial performance claims.
