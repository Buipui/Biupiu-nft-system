# EXP-002 — Olla/Clay-Pot Irrigation: First-Pass Model

**Date:** 2026-09-18
**Status:** Executed as an illustrative sensitivity model; not field-validated.

## Claim under test
Subsurface porous-vessel irrigation may reduce exposed-surface water loss compared with open irrigation, while delivering water near plant roots.

## Model assumptions
- Area: 1 m²
- Period: 30 days
- Illustrative open-surface evaporation: 5 mm/day
- Illustrative mulch/open-surface reduction factor: 0.65
- Illustrative olla delivery: 2 L/day
- 1 mm water over 1 m² = 1 L

## Calculated reference values
- Open-surface water loss: 5 × 30 = **150 L**
- Modeled reduced-loss case: 150 × 0.65 = **97.5 L**
- Olla delivery over period: 2 × 30 = **60 L**

## Interpretation
The model shows that lower exposed evaporation and controlled delivery can reduce the modeled water requirement, but it does **not** establish that an olla system will outperform a specific irrigation method in the field. The assumptions are illustrative and must be replaced with measured soil, climate, crop, vessel, and flow data.

## Result classification
**INCONCLUSIVE for real-world performance.** The model is internally calculable and supports designing a field comparison, but it neither proves universal superiority nor disproves the claim.

## Proposed field validation
Randomized plots comparing open irrigation, mulch-assisted irrigation, and olla irrigation; measure applied water, soil moisture, plant growth, evaporation/weather variables, and yield over multiple irrigation cycles.
