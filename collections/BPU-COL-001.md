## Purpose

The Genesis Collection establishes the first structured Biupiu digital-art collection linking computational geometry, generative visual systems and documented ancient-technology research.

## Collection Principles

- Computational geometry as a creative and research medium.
- Ancient engineering and material culture as research inspiration.
- Clear provenance between concept, artwork, research and NFT metadata.
- Physical signed artworks may be paired with digital editions.
- Public NFT release does not itself establish patentability or legal IP protection.
- Commercial Biupiu DTIC projects remain separate unless explicitly transferred into this portfolio.
