# Biupiu Invention & Concept Register

**Register Version:** 0.1  
**Purpose:** Controlled index for Biupiu inventions, technical concepts, computational-art systems and research-linked IP associated with the NFT / art portfolio.

## Registration Rules

1. Assign a unique record ID before public release.
2. Record the earliest known creation date and version.
3. Separate documented facts from hypotheses or future concepts.
4. Link each NFT/artwork to its underlying concept where applicable.
5. Preserve supporting calculations, source references, sketches and computational outputs.
6. Do not treat an NFT mint as proof of patentability or legal IP protection.
7. Review potentially protectable inventions with qualified IP counsel before public disclosure.
