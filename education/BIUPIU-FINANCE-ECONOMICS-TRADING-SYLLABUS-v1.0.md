# Biupiu World Finance, Economics & Trading Syllabus v1.0

## Philosophy

Teach economics through the Biupiu ecosystem: farming, crops, barter, production, treasury management and then financial markets.

This is educational simulation, not personalised financial advice or a signal service.

## Module 1 — Foundations
- needs vs wants
- scarcity
- opportunity cost
- production
- supply and demand
- inflation
- interest
- risk and return

## Module 2 — Farm Economy
- crop production
- inventory
- barter
- comparative advantage
- pricing
- seasonal supply
- transport and storage costs

## Module 3 — Money & Currency
- why money exists
- money as medium of exchange
- unit of account
- store of value
- currency supply
- velocity
- digital ledgers
- BPU as a future ecosystem settlement asset

## Module 4 — Treasury
- revenue and expenditure
- cash-flow management
- reserves
- budgets
- treasury controls
- allocation policy
- audit trails
- stress testing

## Module 5 — Markets
Paper/simulation only:
- equities
- bonds
- commodities
- currencies/forex
- futures
- options
- crypto assets
- ETFs
- indices
- market microstructure
- liquidity and slippage

## Module 6 — Trading Platforms
Compare interfaces and mechanics using paper accounts:
- charting platforms
- broker platforms
- exchange interfaces
- order books
- market/limit/stop orders
- fees
- custody
- settlement

No live trading is required to complete the course.

## Module 7 — Risk Management
- position sizing
- diversification
- drawdown
- leverage
- liquidation
- counterparty risk
- operational/security risk
- fraud and scam detection

## Module 8 — Trading Laboratory
Students record a hypothesis, entry/exit rules, simulated position, risk, outcome and post-trade analysis.

No system is presented as guaranteed to work.

## Module 9 — Economics Laboratory
Use the Treasury Simulator and Farm Barter Simulator to test:
- monetary policy scenarios
- supply shocks
- treasury allocation
- market liquidity
- barter exchange rates
- production shocks

## Module 10 — Ethics & Regulation
- market integrity
- manipulation vs legitimate trading
- disclosures
- consumer protection
- custody
- AML/KYC concepts
- regulated service providers
- responsible financial education

For South African learners, the syllabus should link to current FSCA consumer-education and licensing information. The FSCA describes consumer education as a way to help people make informed financial decisions and states that CASPs require authorisation to conduct business. 

## Assessment

Students complete:
1. a farm barter scenario
2. a virtual treasury budget
3. a paper-trading journal
4. a risk-management exercise
5. an economic stress test
6. a final integrated Biupiu World simulation

## Resource policy

Third-party videos are learning references, not Biupiu-owned content. Link to originals rather than copying or rehosting them. YouTube notes that creators generally retain copyright in their original videos and provides sharing/linking tools.

Biupiu-produced videos should use original footage, original graphics and the brand's crop/farm/barter visual language.
