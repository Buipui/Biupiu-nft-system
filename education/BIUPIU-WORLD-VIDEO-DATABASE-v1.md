# Biupiu World Learning Video Database

A cross-civilisation educational video index for future Biupiu World episodes.

## Required metadata
- creator/title
- canonical source URL
- topic
- civilisation/environment tags
- evidence classification
- date checked
- rights/usage status
- episode idea
- notes

## Source policy
Prefer museums, universities, archaeological organisations, established educational channels and primary-source repositories. Third-party videos are references unless licensing permits reuse.

## Initial series tracks
- Civilisation agriculture
- Ancient water engineering
- Architecture
- Metallurgy and materials
- Textiles and crafts
- Food systems
- Trade/barter
- Astronomy and calendars
- Archaeological methods
- Experimental archaeology
- Speculative-history methodology

## Cross-environment design
Every existing and future environment gets a Video Library object. The same database can populate the Inka, Maya, Babylonian, Atlantis, Mu and Feudal Japan learning spaces.

## Status
Database schema and editorial workflow are implemented; individual videos are added after source/rights review.
