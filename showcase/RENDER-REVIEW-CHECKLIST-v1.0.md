# Biupiu Render Review Checklist v1.0

## Gate 05 checks
1. Confirm every imported asset has a Biupiu asset ID.
2. Confirm source and licence/provenance records exist.
3. Confirm evidence state and claim class are preserved.
4. Confirm technical overlays are labelled as design/research information where applicable.
5. Confirm no placeholder is presented as a finished product component.
6. Confirm render output is identified as review material until approved.
7. Confirm no unsupported performance claim is embedded in captions, overlays or metadata.

**HOLD** — Gate 05 establishes the first renderable review package but does not claim final rendered product assets.
