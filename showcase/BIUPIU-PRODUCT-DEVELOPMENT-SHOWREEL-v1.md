# Biupiu Product Development Showreel v1

## Purpose
Create a cinematic product-development reel from the same Blender assets used by Biupiu World and the Digital Lab.

## Sequence
1. Biupiu World gateway — 5–8s
2. Regenerative agriculture / smart-farming technology — 8–10s
3. Materials and HempCarbon/composite research — 8–10s
4. Biupiu Blade microturbine hero — 10–12s
5. Marine/AQUA concept — 8–10s
6. AeroBlade GT — 10–12s
7. eVTOL and helicopter/aerospace concepts — 10–12s
8. Robotics/AI laboratory — 6–8s
9. Photonics/optical systems — 6–8s
10. Digital Twin transition — 5–7s
11. Biupiu World multi-department montage — 8–10s
12. Closing Biupiu identity frame — 4–5s

## Product-development visuals
Use hero shots, exploded assemblies, material macros, transparent cutaways, animated data overlays, Digital Twin transitions and workshop/environment context.

## Required labels
CONCEPT; RESEARCH CONCEPT; DESIGN STUDY; PROTOTYPE; DEMONSTRATOR; AVAILABLE PRODUCT only when verified.

## Outputs
16:9 master; 30s trailer; 15s teaser; 9:16 vertical; 4:5 campaign; still-image hero set; World showroom loops.

## Production rule
The showreel is a visualisation and communication product. It must not convert unvalidated engineering concepts into performance claims.
