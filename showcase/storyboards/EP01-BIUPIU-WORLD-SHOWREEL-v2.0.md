# Biupiu World — Episode 01 Showreel Storyboard v2.0

**Runtime:** 90–120 seconds  |  **Primary:** 16:9  |  **Derivatives:** 30s, 15s, 9:16, 4:5, showroom loop
**Status:** Production specification; renders require locally installed Blender/UE5 toolchains and validated assets.

## Creative direction
Premium biotech × regenerative agriculture × advanced materials. Use the authoritative Biupiu gold-on-deep-green logo. Apply stealth-geometric and golden-mean composition. Every non-validated concept is labelled CONCEPT, RESEARCH, or PROTOTYPE.

## Sequence
1. **00:00–00:08 — Gateway / Main Hub**: camera enters Biupiu World; logo reveal; establish campus, research and manufacturing spaces.
2. **00:08–00:20 — Regenerative Agriculture (AGR-01)**: soil systems, biochar, water-retention methods, crop research and farm-to-material pathways.
3. **00:20–00:32 — Materials & Textiles**: HempCarbon, fibres, composites, biodegradable adhesives, textile development and material sample close-ups.
4. **00:32–00:44 — Propulsion**: Biupiu Blade microturbine; technical turntable, airflow overlays and clearly marked prototype status.
5. **00:44–00:56 — Marine / AQUA (MAR-01/MAR-02)**: expedition boat, hydrofoil and sampling interfaces; marine research port fly-through.
6. **00:56–01:08 — Aero / Mobility**: AeroBlade GT, eVTOL, helicopter and aerospace concepts; no claim of engineering validation from visuals alone.
7. **01:08–01:20 — Robotics & AI**: manufacturing cells, digital-twin telemetry, AI intelligence hub and automation control surfaces.
8. **01:20–01:30 — Photonics**: structured light, LiFi/free-space optical communication and optical-computing research visualisations.
9. **01:30–01:45 — Digital Twin / World Montage**: linked data → simulation → visualization → product showroom; rapid cross-space montage.
10. **01:45–02:00 — End Card**: Biupiu logo, “Biotechnology • Regenerative Systems • Advanced Materials • Intelligent Design”, website/CTA placeholder.

## Camera and render rules
- Reusable camera routes: gateway push-in, orbital product turntable, macro material sweep, aerial campus flyover, lab tracking shot.
- Use Blender for asset preparation and approved modelling; use UE5 as external presentation/cinematic runtime.
- Keep canonical research, validated simulation data and provenance records outside the visual scene.
- Third-party models must retain source URL, creator, licence, access date, permitted use and modification status.
- Deliver clean master, logo-safe version, captions/subtitle version and still-frame exports.
