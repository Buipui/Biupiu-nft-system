## Reference
- Source: user-supplied concept board, Biupiu Regenerative Technology Ecosystem.png
- Role: approved design inspiration and silhouette reference for future photorealistic development
- Authority: this reference supplements the authoritative uploaded Biupiu logo; it does not replace the logo master or canonical engineering assets
- Status: DESIGN REFERENCE LOCKED — photorealistic 3D reconstruction and validation pending

## Design intent
Build on the concept board's product family while preserving a coherent Biupiu identity:
- premium biotech × regenerative agriculture × advanced materials
- deep-ocean/deep-green environments with gold accents
- carbon black, graphite grey, solar gold and sage green material accents
- stealth geometry and Golden Mean-inspired proportioning
- streamlined, low-drag silhouettes with controlled surface continuity
- consistent product-family detailing across Blade, Marine, eVTOL, Helicopter and Aerospace
- cinematic photorealism for final renders; concept/CAD/prototype labels where validation is incomplete

## Production rules
1. Use the board as a silhouette, proportion and styling reference; do not treat generated pixels as engineering-accurate geometry.
2. Create or adapt canonical 3D assets before producing final animation shots.
3. Keep Biupiu-original geometry, propulsion modules, materials, interfaces and engineering assumptions in separate source-controlled layers.
4. Maintain asset provenance for all third-party base models and textures.
5. Use the authoritative Biupiu logo asset; do not allow downstream image generation to redraw or alter the wordmark.
6. Apply explicit evidence labels: CONCEPT, RESEARCH, PROTOTYPE, or VALIDATED only when supported by records.
7. Use the same camera, lighting, material and color-management profiles across the showreel.
8. Render stills and turntables for approval before locking longer cinematic sequences.
9. Preserve source-of-truth research, simulation and test data outside the visual scene.

## Render development stages
- Stage A: silhouette and proportion blockout
- Stage B: clean topology / CAD or mesh preparation
- Stage C: material, decal and logo pass
- Stage D: lighting and camera continuity pass
- Stage E: engineering annotation / digital-twin linkage
- Stage F: photorealistic still approval
- Stage G: animation, transitions and derivative exports

## Acceptance checks
- Logo correctness verified
- Family-level visual continuity verified
- Material palette consistent
- No accidental third-party branding
- Licence and provenance recorded
- Concept/research/prototype claims labelled
- Geometry and motion reviewed separately from marketing composition
- Final shot metadata registered in the Episode 01 manifest

## Relationship to showreel
This lock is a prerequisite for the Episode 01 showreel but does not authorize final rendering by itself. Blender/UE5 and renderer integrations must be available and validated before the production render gate is marked complete.
