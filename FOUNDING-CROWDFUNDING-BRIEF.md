# BIUPIU — Founding NFT Collection & Research Crowdfunding Project

## Project Overview

Biupiu is an emerging South African research and innovation initiative exploring the intersection of biotechnology, regenerative agriculture, ancient technology, computational engineering, materials science, energy systems, plant research and computational geometry.

The Biupiu Founding NFT Collection is an initial crowdfunding mechanism intended to help establish the Biupiu research and development ecosystem.

The NFTs are designed as more than ordinary digital collectibles. Each artwork may be connected to a research concept, computational process, historical technology, scientific visualization or engineering exploration.

## Vision

Biupiu explores how modern science and computation can investigate ideas inspired by nature, ancient technologies and mathematical structures in the world around us.

Research themes include ancient engineering, archaeology, computational geometry, biological structures, regenerative agriculture, plant science, hemp and biomaterials, water systems, energy systems, materials engineering, aerodynamics, computational modelling and generative art.

Documented evidence, scientific interpretation, engineering models and creative speculation are kept distinct.

## Ancient Systems × Computational Geometry

A core Biupiu artwork stream will investigate documented ancient technologies and archaeological systems through modern computational methods.

Potential themes include ancient irrigation, agricultural terraces, waru-waru systems, water management, mining, metallurgy, ceramics, stone construction, architectural geometry, archaeoastronomy, transportation and trade networks, southern African archaeological technologies and ancient hydraulic systems.

Where evidence permits, Biupiu may construct computational reconstructions. Mathematical models can then become inputs to original generative artworks.

## Research-to-Art Pipeline

Research → historical/scientific source material → data/measurements → mathematical model → computational geometry → algorithm → generative process → original artwork → NFT metadata → blockchain record.

Each computational artwork may preserve the algorithm, software version, parameters and generation seed where appropriate.

## Evidence Classification

- DOCUMENTED — supported by reliable historical, archaeological or scientific evidence.
- RECONSTRUCTED — computational or physical reconstruction based on available evidence.
- EXPERIMENTAL — Biupiu experiment testing a proposed mechanism or model.
- HYPOTHESIS — proposed interpretation requiring further investigation.
- SPECULATIVE — creative exploration inspired by a subject rather than an established conclusion.

## Crowdfunding Purpose

Funds raised may contribute toward establishing Biupiu research and development infrastructure, including research and development, computational resources, software and development, laboratory and experimental development, agricultural and materials research, prototyping, digital infrastructure, research documentation, generative artwork development, website and archive development, intellectual-property development and technical resources.

## Supporter / Collector Position

Depending on the edition, supporters may receive original Biupiu digital artwork, an NFT token, edition information, provenance information, computational-generation information where applicable, access to publicly released project documentation and recognition as an early supporter.

Specific benefits are defined by each NFT release.

## Intellectual Property

Ownership of an NFT token does not automatically transfer ownership of Biupiu patents, inventions, trademarks, proprietary research, confidential information, biological materials, source code or underlying scientific discoveries. Each release will state its applicable NFT licence.

Third-party research, software, datasets, images and historical materials will only be incorporated according to applicable licences or permissions.

Potentially patentable or IP-sensitive information may be withheld from public repositories until appropriate protection has been considered.

## Long-Term Ecosystem

The founding collection is intended as the first layer of a larger Biupiu ecosystem spanning research, computational engineering, ancient systems, regenerative agriculture, materials, energy, digital art and a research-linked NFT archive.

## Founding Statement

The Biupiu Founding NFT Collection represents an experiment in connecting research, computation, art and community support.

Can research become art, can art communicate research, and can a community help fund the development of new scientific and technological ideas?

Biupiu intends to explore that question by combining ancient knowledge, modern science, computational geometry, biological systems and creative technology.

The first NFTs represent the beginning of the Biupiu vision.
