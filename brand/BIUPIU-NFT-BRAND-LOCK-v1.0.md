# BIUPIU NFT Brand Lock v1.0

**Effective:** 2026-09-16  
**Scope:** All Biupiu NFT artwork, collection covers, certificates, collector pages, metadata presentation and promotional visuals.

## Master brand reference

The two user-supplied BIUPIU brand images dated 2026-09-16 are the visual master references for NFT presentation:

1. BIUPIU wide banner / brand lock-up: `1000064572.jpg`
2. BIUPIU logo / vertical brand lock-up: `1000064427.jpg`

The BIUPIU emblem is the DNA + leaf + orbital-node mark shown in these master references. Use the approved mark consistently; do not substitute generic leaf, DNA, atom or mandala logos.

## Brand expression

**Brand name:** BIUPIU  
**Tagline family:** Plant-Based Biotechnology & Regenerative Innovation / Science Rooted in a Better Tomorrow  
**Core aesthetic:** premium biotechnology × regenerative innovation × advanced materials × computational science.

## NFT composition rules

- Use the approved BIUPIU logo/emblem in the header or a clearly subordinate branded zone.
- Keep the BIUPIU wordmark legible and unobstructed.
- Preserve the deep forest / emerald base with restrained gold accents.
- Teal/aqua is reserved for water, light, flow fields, energy or computational phenomena.
- Gold should feel metallic and premium, not bright yellow.
- Botanical and DNA motifs may support the biotechnology identity but must not replace the logo.
- Artwork remains the hero; branding frames the research object rather than overwhelming it.
- Ancient-system artwork may use archaeological geometry, terrain, water, stone, fibres, metals or historical process cues, but unsupported claims must not be visually presented as established fact.

## Banner standard

For collection pages, certificates and presentation graphics, the wide BIUPIU banner should establish the identity before the NFT-specific artwork panel. The banner should retain the dark green photographic/scientific atmosphere, gold BIUPIU identity, plant-based biotechnology statement and restrained technical motifs from the supplied master reference.

## ORIGIN-001 integration

`BIU-NFT-0001` uses this brand lock with the visual concept **Ancient Water / Computational Flow I**. The hero artwork may combine water-flow geometry, botanical structure, terrain contours, radial/recursive computational geometry and refined gold linework.

## Negative rules

Do not use:

- generic green “eco” clip-art branding;
- unrelated logos or replacement emblems;
- rainbow/neon branding that breaks the palette;
- fantasy ruins presented as archaeology;
- unsupported “sacred geometry” claims;
- fake blockchain/token data in final metadata;
- AI-generated text as authoritative metadata.

## Final-use rule

Every future BIUPIU NFT generation prompt or design brief must reference this brand lock before artwork generation. The final technical metadata must come from the repository records, not from generated artwork text.
