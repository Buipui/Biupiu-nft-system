# Biupiu Speculative Space — Atlantis & Mu v1.0

## Atlantis

### Evidence baseline
Atlantis is primarily known through Plato's Timaeus and Critias. Plato describes an island beyond the Pillars of Heracles, a fertile central plain, concentric land-and-water arrangements, springs, agriculture and a later destruction/submergence narrative. These are features of the Platonic account, not independently verified archaeological facts.

### Scenario architecture
1. Platonic Core — reproduce the geography described in Critias as a literary-source scenario.
2. Agricultural Plain — model fertile lowland agriculture, water allocation, storage and food surplus.
3. Concentric Capital — model alternating land/water rings and controlled movement.
4. Water & Springs — model the warm/cold spring motif.
5. Maritime Network — model hypothetical island-to-island trade.
6. Catastrophe Scenario — optional earthquake/flood/submergence simulation, explicitly labelled a scenario.
7. Alternative-location research — hypotheses remain separate branches.

Evidence label: LITERARY_TRADITION / RECONSTRUCTION
Confidence: OPEN

## Mu

Mu is treated as a speculative/modern lost-continent tradition rather than an established archaeological civilisation. The system does not assign a confirmed location, date, technology set, genetic lineage or historical population.

### Scenario architecture
1. Mu Hypothesis Island — procedurally generated island/archipelago with parameterised location.
2. Environmental Model — ocean, rainfall, freshwater and geological variables.
3. Agriculture Module — configurable hypothetical terraces, raised beds, forest gardens and water management.
4. Settlement Module — procedural settlement patterns without historical-accuracy claims.
5. Maritime Module — hypothetical inter-island exchange.
6. Collapse Module — optional geological/climate/disaster scenarios.
7. Hypothesis Branching — multiple layouts can coexist.

Evidence label: SPECULATIVE_TRADITION / HYPOTHESIS
Confidence: OPEN

## Speculative Atlas

Four layers:
- L0 Evidence — source/evidence classification.
- L1 Tradition — literary/mythological descriptions.
- L2 Reconstruction — visual/architectural/agricultural interpretation.
- L3 Simulation — playable parameterised world.

Moving between layers never upgrades evidentiary status.

## Shared Biupiu systems
Atlantis and Mu connect to the civilisation farming engine, crop/water simulation, farm barter simulator, treasury/education simulator, R&D digital laboratory, provenance/trust records and Biupiu World exploration.

All economy values here are virtual simulation units unless separately activated as a production economy.
