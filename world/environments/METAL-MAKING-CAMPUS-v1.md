# Biupiu World — Metal Making Campus

The main hub now separates two educational destinations.

## Campus / Main Hub
Avatar Arrival -> Campus Plaza -> Choose Destination

### Farming World
Agriculture, water engineering, crops, regenerative systems, barter and food economies.

### Metal Making World
Metallurgy, materials science, historical metalworking, experimental simulation and the digital materials laboratory.

## Metal-making spaces
- Japan: tatara/tamahagane comparative workshop
- India: wootz/crucible-steel research workshop
- Damascus: crucible-steel trade and metalworking study
- General Materials Lab: cross-civilisation comparison
- Modern Biupiu Materials Lab: non-historical R&D, separately labelled

Historical spaces must not silently import modern Biupiu research as historical fact.
