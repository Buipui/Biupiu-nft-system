# Biupiu World — Japanese Sword Workshop

## Educational purpose
A virtual historical-metallurgy workshop teaching material selection, forging concepts, heat treatment, folding/forge-welding, grinding, polishing and pattern formation.

## Important terminology
Traditional Japanese swordsmithing used **tamahagane**, steel produced in a tatara furnace from iron-rich sand. Wootz steel is a different historical crucible-steel tradition and must not be presented as the material traditionally used for Japanese swords. The workshop therefore has two clearly separated learning tracks:

1. **Japanese tamahagane track** — historically grounded.
2. **Wootz comparative metallurgy track** — compare crucible steel with Japanese tatara steel and investigate differences experimentally in a safe virtual lab.

## Safety boundary
This is a virtual/educational simulation. It does not provide operational instructions for making weapons or live blades.
