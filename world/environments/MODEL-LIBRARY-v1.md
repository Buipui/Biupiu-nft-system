## Inka
Use steep terrain as the primary design constraint. Terraces use retaining walls and layered drainage/soil construction; water channels and fountains are integrated into the terrain. ## Maya

## Babylonian
Use the existing repository's evidence-grounded alluvial irrigation environment as the baseline. Garden/urban reconstructions must carry their evidence classification.

## Atlantis and Mu
Use the Speculative Atlas rules. Models are scenario assets, not archaeological claims.

## Asset rule
Models are specifications until a rendering/3D asset exists. Source references and reconstruction status travel with the asset manifest.
