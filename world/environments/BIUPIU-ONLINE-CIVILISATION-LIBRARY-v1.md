# Biupiu Online Civilisation Library

The online library is the information layer attached to each playable environment.

## Library rooms
1. Welcome / orientation
2. Historical evidence
3. Architecture and engineering
4. Agriculture and water
5. Daily-life objects
6. Trade/barter
7. Digital reconstruction notes
8. Research questions
9. Source catalogue
10. Simulation results

## Avatar interaction
Avatars can approach a library object, receive an evidence card, inspect the source classification, and then enter the associated simulation.

## Access model
Public educational content can be browsed by subscribers. Research notes can be versioned and provenance-tracked. Restricted/private R&D remains outside public environment packages.

## Accuracy rule
The library must distinguish established evidence, scholarly interpretation, reconstruction and speculation before an avatar enters a reconstructed space.
