## Production rule
Each environment is a self-contained Blender scene/collection with environment_id, evidence_state, provenance, source references, licence status, LOD targets, lighting profile, navigation/collision profile and Digital Twin links.

## Optimisation
Desktop scenes may use high-detail geometry, PBR materials, volumetrics and animation. Android receives LOD2/LOD3 derivatives through the existing cross-platform packaging process.

## Historical/speculative separation
Documented/reconstructed environments and speculative environments remain separate in scene metadata and presentation. Speculative material is labelled as such.
