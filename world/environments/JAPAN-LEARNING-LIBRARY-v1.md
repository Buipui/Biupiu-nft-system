# Feudal Japan Learning Library

## Core modules
1. Sengoku-period timeline
2. Castle towns and settlement planning
3. Rice agriculture and irrigation
4. Forestry and mountain ecology
5. Samurai material culture
6. Craft guilds and workshops
7. Japanese sword metallurgy
8. Tatara and tamahagane
9. Comparative wootz metallurgy
10. Tea, ceramics, textiles and paper
11. Trade and marketplace systems
12. Primary-source and museum evidence
13. Digital reconstruction methodology
14. Virtual farming/barter simulation

Every module receives evidence classification and source metadata.

## Video database
Videos are indexed, not copied. Each record stores title, creator, source URL, topic, date checked, rights/usage status, evidence class, educational value and intended Biupiu episode.

The database is designed to supply future Biupiu World educational series episodes across all environments.
