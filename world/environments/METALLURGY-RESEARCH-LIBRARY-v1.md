## Historical comparison

Japan: tatara, tamahagane and traditional swordmaking context.

India: crucible steel / wootz, ingot production, South Indian origins and trade, Deccan metalworking.

Damascus: historical use of crucible steel and regional metalworking; distinguish historical watered/crucible steel from modern pattern-welded products.
