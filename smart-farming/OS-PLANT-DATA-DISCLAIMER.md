# Biupiu Farming System OS — Plant Data & Optimisation Disclaimer

**Version:** 0.1
**Purpose:** Product/software architecture and user-facing notice for plant-growth data logging.

Biupiu Farming System OS may allow users to select plants/crops, connect compatible sensors and products, record observations, and submit anonymised or account-linked growing data to Biupiu infrastructure where the user has enabled that function and applicable terms/privacy notices permit it.

## Important limitations
- The OS provides monitoring, analytics and optimisation recommendations; it does not guarantee crop yield, plant health or biological outcomes.
- Recommendations are dependent on sensor accuracy, calibration, cultivar/genetics, substrate/soil, climate, water quality, management practices and other environmental variables.
- Plant profiles are starting datasets and must not be treated as universal prescriptions for every cultivar or location.
- Users remain responsible for lawful cultivation, product use, irrigation, fertilisation, pest management and other agricultural decisions.
- Automated actions must remain bounded by configured safety limits and compatible hardware protections.
- Biupiu may use appropriately governed telemetry and experiment results to improve models, plant profiles, products and optimisation protocols. Data handling must follow the applicable privacy notice, consent choices, retention rules and data-protection requirements.
- Users should not enter sensitive personal information into plant experiment notes unless the system explicitly requests and protects it.

## Experimental-data principle
The OS should distinguish **measured observations**, **user-entered observations**, **model-derived recommendations**, **experimental hypotheses**, and **validated protocols**. A recommendation must not automatically become a validated protocol merely because it produced one successful result.
