# SF-07 Campus Prototype Guide

1. Open the Biupiu campus.
2. Enter the Farming Systems District.
3. Select Waru Waru Learning Space.
4. Explore the Overview station.
5. Open Water Flow or Thermal Simulation.
6. Choose a Digital Laboratory experiment.
7. Run the simulation.
8. Save the experiment.
9. Return to the facility and open the Research station.
10. Continue to the related Academy lesson.

The prototype is educational and simulation-based. It does not establish historical, biological or engineering claims merely because a model produces a result.
