# Biupiu Online OS — Getting Started

1. Create or sign into an account.
2. Open the Dashboard.
3. Choose Research Library, Digital Laboratory, Plant Intelligence or Courses.
4. Select a course or experiment.
5. Follow the lesson and practical activity.
6. Use the AI Assistant for explanations and experiment support.
7. Save your work and review evidence labels.
8. Continue to the next lesson or experiment.
