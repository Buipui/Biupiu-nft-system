# Biupiu World Property & Market User Guide

1. Enter an environment.
2. Browse Homes or the local Market.
3. Open an item and inspect its description, context, rights and availability.
4. Complete checkout when live commerce is enabled.
5. Confirm the item appears under My Entitlements.
6. Return to the environment and access eligible content.
7. Use My Home, My Avatar Items and My Gallery to manage your digital possessions.
