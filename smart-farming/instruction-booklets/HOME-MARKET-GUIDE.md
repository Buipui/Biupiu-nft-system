# Biupiu World Homes & Markets — Visitor Guide

1. Choose an environment from the Campus Map.
2. Explore available homes and public spaces.
3. Open a property to view its design, features and context.
4. Use the Home Configurator to customise eligible features.
5. Visit the environment's dedicated market.
6. Inspect provenance/licence information for historical-inspired products.
7. Purchase or licence products when the live commerce system is enabled.
8. Access owned/licensed digital spaces from your account.

Historical-inspired architecture is a digital reconstruction or interpretation unless explicitly identified otherwise.
