# Biupiu World Marketplace — User Guide

1. Enter the Biupiu Campus.
2. Visit the Store or Gallery Shop.
3. Browse contemporary avatar items, artwork or other available products.
4. Open an item to review its description, provenance/licence and availability.
5. Add eligible items to the account/cart flow.
6. Complete checkout through the authorised commerce system.
7. Digital items become available through the user's entitlements after confirmation.
8. Physical orders follow the applicable fulfilment and returns process.

Virtual displays are not themselves proof of product availability; the commerce catalogue is authoritative.
