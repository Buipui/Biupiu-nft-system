# Biupiu Online Laboratory — User Guide v0.1

## Getting started
1. Sign in to Biupiu OS.
2. Confirm your subscription access.
3. Open Research Library or Digital Laboratory.
4. Search for a topic or choose an experiment template.
5. Review the evidence level.
6. Define your hypothesis and variables.
7. Run the digital experiment.
8. Review and save results.
9. Ask the Biupiu Intelligence Assistant for interpretation or next-step ideas.
10. Export or share the experiment according to your permissions.

## Remember
A simulation is not proof of a physical result. Preserve the distinction between simulation, observation and validated experimental evidence.
