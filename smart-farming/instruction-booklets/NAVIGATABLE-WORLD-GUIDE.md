# Biupiu Navigable World — Visitor Guide

1. Open the Biupiu map.
2. Choose a district.
3. Enter a facility.
4. Inspect the learning stations.
5. Open research cards for background information.
6. Watch the linked lesson.
7. Launch the associated Digital Laboratory experiment.
8. Save your result.
9. Discuss or share where your permissions allow.
10. Return to the Academy to continue the learning path.

Historical reconstructions are educational digital environments. Labels identify documented information, reconstruction choices, simulations and hypotheses separately.
