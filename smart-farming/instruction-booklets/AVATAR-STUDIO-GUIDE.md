# Biupiu Avatar Studio — User Guide

1. Open Avatar Studio from the Biupiu Campus.
2. Select a base avatar.
3. Choose contemporary Biupiu styling or a historical-inspired research pack.
4. Inspect the context/provenance information for historical items.
5. Customise clothing, accessories and colours.
6. Save your avatar.
7. Enter the Campus, Academy, Laboratory or Community spaces.
8. Change your avatar whenever you choose.

Historical-inspired options are educational reconstructions and do not identify the user's ancestry or ethnicity.
