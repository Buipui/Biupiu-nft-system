# SF-21 — Biupiu World Content Studio

Date: 2026-09-18

## Objective
Make Biupiu World a reusable virtual production environment for multiple future video series. The World is a persistent visual identity and production stage, not a one-off advertisement.

## Core principle
Build the world once, then continuously produce new episodes, campaigns, product launches, research explainers, Academy lessons and cinematic stories inside the same environment.

## Persistent production layers
1. World geometry
2. Department districts
3. Showcase vehicles/products
4. Lighting and weather presets
5. Biupiu branding
6. Camera routes
7. Character/avatar layer
8. Research/education overlays
9. Advertising surfaces
10. Audio layer
11. Episode metadata
12. Rights/provenance layer

## Video series framework

### SERIES-01 — Biupiu World
Cinematic tours, new districts, vehicles and world updates.

### SERIES-02 — Biupiu Department Showcase
One department/product/vehicle per episode.

### SERIES-03 — Digital Lab
Experiments, simulations, hypotheses and results. Claims must be labelled by evidence status.

### SERIES-04 — Biupiu Academy
Courses, demonstrations, tutorials and virtual classes.

### SERIES-05 — Regenerative Farming
Waru Waru, water systems, crops, smart farming, sensors and field experiments.

### SERIES-06 — Biotech & Materials
Plant research, fibres, composites, adhesives, biochemical research and advanced materials.

### SERIES-07 — Intelligence Hub
AI, robotics, automation, software architecture and digital infrastructure.

### SERIES-08 — Mobility & Marine
EVTOL, aircraft, hydrofoils, utility vehicles and future mobility concepts.

### SERIES-09 — Ancient Technology Research
Historically grounded reconstructions and clearly labelled engineering interpretations.

### SERIES-10 — Biupiu Product Studio
Product design, prototypes, manufacturing concepts and future commercial products.

### SERIES-11 — World Stories
Avatar-led exploration, community stories and educational journeys.

### SERIES-12 — Launch & Campaign
Product launches, investor presentations, announcements and campaign films.

## Episode template
HOOK
→ WORLD ESTABLISHING SHOT
→ DEPARTMENT/LOCATION
→ HERO PRODUCT OR VEHICLE
→ EXPLANATION / STORY
→ RESEARCH OR DEMONSTRATION
→ RELATED DIGITAL LAB / ACADEMY CONTENT
→ CALL TO ACTION
→ NEXT EPISODE / WORLD LOCATION

## Reusable scene IDs
AIR-01 Advanced Mobility Hangar
MAR-01 Marine Research Port
AGR-01 Regenerative Farm Gateway
CONS-01 Conservation Expedition Base
AI-01 Intelligence Hub Showroom

These remain persistent locations and can appear in many different episodes.

## Video variants
Every approved episode should be renderable as:
- cinematic 16:9
- mobile 9:16
- short-form 1:1 or 4:5 where appropriate
- World in-game sequence
- Academy lesson
- product advertisement
- investor/presentation cut

## Production rule
Do not rebuild the world for every video. Reuse stable scene IDs, assets and camera routes; update only the episode content and approved assets.

## Rights rule
User-supplied/original Biupiu material and explicitly licensed assets may enter production after provenance review. Research-only or restricted game assets remain reference-only.
