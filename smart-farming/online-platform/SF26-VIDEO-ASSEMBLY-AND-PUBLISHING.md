Convert the SF-25 previsualisation package into an edit-ready, rights-aware publication pipeline for **Biupiu World — Episode 01: Welcome to Biupiu World**.

**Status:** EDIT-READY / PREVIS ONLY  
**Important:** This specification does not claim that a finished animated video has been rendered or published.

## Edit language
- Use restrained cinematic dissolves, match cuts and environmental transitions.
- Preserve geographic continuity so the five districts feel like one persistent world.
- Use subtle Biupiu identity elements rather than generic green-tech graphics.
- Keep department/status labels readable on mobile and desktop.
- No direct imitation of game cinematics or use of unlicensed game assets.

## Audio
- Original or properly licensed cinematic score only.
- Layer environmental ambience by district.
- Keep narration optional so the master can be repurposed internationally.
- Record narration/captions as separate tracks.
- Do not use copyrighted game soundtrack material.
