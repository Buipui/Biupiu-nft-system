# Biupiu Community Spatial Layer — Future Architecture

## Long-term vision
A navigable digital Biupiu environment where members can create avatars, move through themed spaces, interact with other members and access research, education and Digital Laboratory functions.

## Proposed spaces
- Biupiu Campus
- Research Library
- Digital Laboratory
- Farming Systems Lab
- Materials Lab
- Gallery/Genesis Art space
- Community Forum
- Training Centre
- Project Showcase

## Avatar capabilities
- Identity/profile representation
- Movement/navigation
- Text/voice communication subject to platform controls
- Join collaborative experiments
- Visit research exhibits
- Attend live or recorded training
- View project showcases

## Phased implementation
Phase A: conventional web OS + accounts.
Phase B: interactive 2D campus/navigation.
Phase C: real-time multi-user spaces and avatars.
Phase D: optional 3D/immersive experience.

The spatial layer should remain optional so the core research and farming functions remain accessible on ordinary phones, tablets and computers.
