# Course 01 — Introduction to Biupiu OS

## Learning outcomes
Students can navigate the platform, search the research library, create a Digital Laboratory experiment and understand evidence levels.

## Lessons
1. What is Biupiu OS?
2. Dashboard and account
3. Research Library
4. Plant Intelligence
5. Digital Laboratory
6. AI Assistant and research discipline

## Practical
Create a simulated plant experiment, save the result and identify which statements are observations, simulations, hypotheses and validated evidence.

## Assessment
10-question knowledge check + practical experiment submission.
