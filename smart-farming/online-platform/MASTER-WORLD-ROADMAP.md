# Biupiu World Master Roadmap v0.1

## Layer 1 — Core OS
Farming OS, Intelligence Hub, Plant Intelligence, device integration.

## Layer 2 — Research
Research Library, Digital Laboratory, evidence/provenance system.

## Layer 3 — Academy
Courses, video series, assessments and learning paths.

## Layer 4 — Navigable World
Campus, facilities, historical research environments and community spaces.

## Layer 5 — Identity
Avatar Studio, contemporary Biupiu styles and cultural research-inspired avatar packs.

## Layer 6 — Commerce
Gallery, avatar shop, homes, environment markets and special editions.

## Layer 7 — Community
Profiles, collaboration, events, future multiplayer spaces and optional 3D/immersive interface.

## Product principle
All layers should use stable IDs and shared account/entitlement services so the world can evolve from a mobile/web experience into a richer 3D environment without rebuilding the research and commerce data layer.
