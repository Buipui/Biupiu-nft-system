# SF-10 — Biupiu Contemporary Style, Gallery & Marketplace Architecture

## Purpose
Add a commercial layer to the Biupiu World for contemporary avatar items, digital/physical artwork and future special-edition campus products.

## Contemporary Biupiu Style Shop
Categories:
- Biupiu avatar clothing
- Contemporary accessories
- Campus badges/insignia
- Researcher/lab attire
- Digital wallpapers and design assets
- Limited-edition avatar items

Each item has: product ID, title, description, media, price/currency, inventory model, entitlement, licence terms, version and status.

## Digital Gallery
A curated gallery can display Biupiu artworks and research-inspired creative works.

Artwork records should contain:
- artwork ID
- title
- artist/creator attribution
- description
- provenance
- edition/availability
- media/preview
- price/status
- rights/licence information
- related research/facility tags

The gallery should distinguish **viewing**, **licensing**, **digital purchase**, and **physical purchase** where applicable.

## Future Campus Marketplace
The campus can eventually contain physical/virtual storefronts where customers discover special Biupiu products.

Example spaces:
- Biupiu Design Store
- Research Instruments Store
- Materials & Prototype Store
- Art Gallery Shop
- Limited Editions Vault

## Commerce principles
- Product availability and price must be fetched from the authoritative commerce service.
- Purchases must not be represented as complete until payment/order confirmation is received.
- Digital licences must state what the buyer receives and may do with it.
- Physical goods require inventory, shipping and returns workflows.
- Marketplace sellers require identity, rights and moderation controls.
