# Biupiu Digital Gallery UI v0.1

## Gallery entrance
Featured works | Collections | Artists | Research-inspired works | Available works

## Artwork page
Hero artwork -> title -> creator -> provenance -> story -> related research/facility -> availability -> purchase/licence action.

## Campus integration
Artwork can appear as an exhibit inside a physical-looking virtual gallery. Selecting it opens the artwork page without leaving the Biupiu World.

## Collection system
Collections can be linked to Academy courses, Genesis/art projects, historical research and individual campus facilities.
