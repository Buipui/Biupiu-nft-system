# Biupiu Academy — Online Course Catalogue v0.1

## Course 01 — Introduction to Biupiu OS
Audience: beginners
Format: 6 lessons + practical lab
Outcome: navigate the OS, library and Digital Laboratory.

## Course 02 — Smart Farming Foundations
Audience: farmers, growers and technology learners
Format: 8 lessons + sensor simulation
Outcome: understand soil, climate, sensor data and evidence-based optimisation.

## Course 03 — Build Your First Smart Farm Node
Audience: makers and technical learners
Format: 8 lessons + Arduino/Raspberry Pi lab
Outcome: assemble a monitored prototype and understand telemetry.

## Course 04 — AI-Assisted Farming
Audience: growers, researchers and technologists
Format: 8 lessons + AI experiment lab
Outcome: use AI to structure hypotheses, interpret measurements and document experiments.

## Course 05 — Plant Intelligence & Growth-Cycle Optimisation
Audience: advanced growers/researchers
Format: 10 lessons + plant-profile project
Outcome: construct a measurable growth-cycle experiment and version optimisation protocols.

## Course 06 — Regenerative Farming Technology
Audience: agriculture and sustainability learners
Format: 8 lessons + systems-design project
Outcome: connect regenerative principles with measurable field data and technology.

## Course 07 — Biocomposites & Agricultural Product Design
Audience: makers, designers and product developers
Format: 8 lessons + materials design lab
Outcome: understand natural-fibre composite product development, testing and prototyping.

## Course 08 — Digital Research Laboratory
Audience: researchers and advanced learners
Format: 6 lessons + capstone experiment
Outcome: formulate hypotheses, run simulations, analyse results and maintain research provenance.

## Course 09 — Build a Smart Greenhouse
Audience: intermediate technical learners
Format: 10 lessons + simulated control project
Outcome: design a sensor/control architecture with safety boundaries.

## Course 10 — From Prototype to Agricultural Product
Audience: entrepreneurs and product developers
Format: 8 lessons + product-development project
Outcome: translate a prototype into specifications, testing gates, documentation and a product roadmap.
