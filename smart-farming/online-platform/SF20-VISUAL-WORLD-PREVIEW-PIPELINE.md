# SF-20 — Visual World Preview Pipeline

Date: 2026-09-18

## Purpose
Connect SF-19 showroom scenes to a visual-preview workflow so Biupiu can review environments, renders, vehicles and advertising layouts before full World Runtime implementation.

## Preview sequence
1. Department scene specification
2. Original concept/render generation
3. Visual identity pass
4. Showroom composition
5. Camera storyboard
6. Preview video
7. Human review
8. Approved asset/provenance registration
9. World Runtime package

## First preview
- Advanced Mobility Hangar
- Marine Research Port
- Regenerative Farm Gateway
- Conservation Expedition Base
- AI/Intelligence Hub Showroom

## Video rules
Preview videos are concept visualisations unless linked to verified production assets. They may show proposed architecture, vehicles and advertising layouts but must not imply that a concept already exists physically.

## Future inputs
Future user-supplied videos, product renders, CAD-derived imagery and approved licensed assets can replace concept imagery while retaining the same scene/camera structure.

## Output targets
- 16:9 World/desktop preview
- 9:16 mobile/social preview
- still hero renders
- showroom thumbnails
- department campaign variants
