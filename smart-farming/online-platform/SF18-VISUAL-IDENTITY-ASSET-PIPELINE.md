# SF-18 — Biupiu Visual Identity Asset Pipeline

Date: 2026-09-18

## Objective
Make future video captures, product-design references, renders, prototypes and licensed 3D assets usable as inputs to a controlled Biupiu advertising-identity pipeline.

## Asset sources
Future inputs may include:
- Biupiu-produced videos and product demonstrations
- original product photography
- original CAD/3D designs
- original concept renders
- approved licensed third-party assets
- research/reference footage
- department-specific visual material

Every external asset must pass the existing provenance/licence gate before production use.

## Transformation pipeline
SOURCE
→ provenance record
→ rights/licence classification
→ visual extraction
→ product/department tagging
→ colour/material analysis
→ composition analysis
→ approved asset generation
→ PBR/3D optimisation
→ advertising template
→ World showcase

## Video-to-identity extraction
Future videos can be analysed for:
- recurring product shapes
- silhouettes
- surface/material language
- lighting
- camera angles
- environmental context
- department colour cues
- logo placement
- typography zones
- advertising compositions
- vehicle/product presentation patterns

The extraction creates **design references and original advertising assets**, not a licence to reproduce third-party copyrighted footage or models.

## Visual identity layers
1. Biupiu master identity
2. department identity
3. product identity
4. vehicle identity
5. environment identity
6. campaign identity
7. World showcase identity

## Material direction
Use physically based materials for consistent product presentation. Relevant properties include base colour, metallic, roughness, normal, emission, clearcoat, transmission and other PBR extensions. citeturn0search2

## Advertising rule
A reference asset can influence the **design language**, but the final Biupiu advertising asset must be:
- original Biupiu work,
- explicitly licensed for intended use,
- or otherwise documented as permitted.
