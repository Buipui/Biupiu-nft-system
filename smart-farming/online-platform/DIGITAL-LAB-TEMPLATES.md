# SF-08 Digital Laboratory Templates

## waru-water-flow-v1
Variables: channel geometry, water input, terrain assumptions. Output: simulated flow indicators. All outputs labelled simulation.

## waru-thermal-v1
Variables: bed/channel geometry, environmental assumptions, water presence. Output: modelled temperature indicators. All outputs labelled simulation.

## waru-crop-v1
Variables: plant profile, growth stage, moisture scenario and environmental inputs. Output: simulated scenario comparison. Not a biological guarantee.
