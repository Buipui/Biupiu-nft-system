# SF-11 — Biupiu World Homes, Environments & Cultural Markets

## Vision
Create residential spaces across the navigable Biupiu World, including historically inspired environments and a contemporary Biupiu district. Homes are optional digital properties that can be purchased/licensed for use in the world.

## Property model
Property = environment + home design + interior package + ownership/licence + access permissions + optional marketplace inventory.

Property types:
- Starter habitat
- Researcher residence
- Premium residence
- Heritage-inspired residence
- Campus penthouse / showcase home
- Community residence

## Environment districts
Initial roadmap:
- Nile Valley / Ancient Egypt-inspired district
- Mesopotamian district: Sumer, Akkad, Babylon and Assyria as distinct research contexts
- Ancient Levant / Phoenician-inspired district
- Ancient Greek-inspired district
- Ancient Roman-inspired district
- Achaemenid Persian-inspired district
- Indus Valley-inspired district
- Ancient China-inspired district
- Ancient India-inspired district
- Mesoamerican district with distinct Maya and Mexica/Aztec research contexts
- Andean district with Tiwanaku and Inca research contexts
- Nubia/Kush-inspired district
- Southern African archaeological-context district
- West African historical-context district where source research supports a specific environment
- Contemporary Biupiu district

These are digital educational environments, not claims that a single house represents an entire civilization.

## Dedicated markets
Every environment can have a local marketplace with:
- environment-specific clothing and avatar items;
- decorative objects;
- architecture-inspired digital furnishings;
- educational artefacts/reproductions;
- artwork;
- course packs;
- research-linked digital collectibles;
- approved physical products where available.

## Cultural integrity
Historical environments must distinguish documented evidence, reconstruction, interpretation and fictional/game-design additions. Products must not be presented as authentic archaeological artefacts unless provenance genuinely establishes authenticity.

## Property rights
Digital property sales must define whether the customer receives ownership, a time-limited licence, access rights or another entitlement. The commerce service remains authoritative for price, availability and transaction status.
