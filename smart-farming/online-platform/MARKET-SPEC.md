# Environment Market Specification v0.1

Every environment receives a dedicated storefront connected to the central commerce catalogue.

## Market navigation
Environment entrance -> Market -> Collections -> Item -> Context/Provenance -> Related course/research -> purchase/licence.

## Market categories
- Homes & property accessories
- Avatar fashion
- Interior/decor
- Art
- Educational resources
- Digital collectibles
- Special editions

## Governance
Market items require publication review, rights/licensing metadata and evidence/context labels where historical material is involved. The system must prevent unverified objects from being marketed as authentic antiquities.
