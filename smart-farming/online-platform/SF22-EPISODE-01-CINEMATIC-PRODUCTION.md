## Purpose
Create the first reusable cinematic episode introducing Biupiu World and its five persistent showcase environments.

Secondary cuts: 30s, 15s, 9:16 short-form, presentation cut

A cinematic journey begins at the Biupiu World gateway and moves through advanced mobility, marine research, regenerative agriculture, conservation and the Intelligence Hub. Each location reveals a department and its showcase vehicle.

## Visual direction
Premium biotech × regenerative agriculture × advanced materials.
Use cinematic architectural CGI, physically based materials, controlled reflections, natural textures, sophisticated lighting and restrained branding.

Avoid generic green-tech aesthetics and avoid direct imitation of GTA, Forza, Battlefield, Arma, Hell Let Loose or other game art.

## Audio direction
Original/licensed cinematic score.
Ambient layers:
- wind
- water
- farm ambience
- subtle machinery
- laboratory ambience
- restrained electronic/technical textures

No copyrighted game soundtrack.

## Production principle
Episode 01 is a reusable template. Future episodes can replace the hero vehicle, department message, camera route or story while keeping the same persistent World locations.
