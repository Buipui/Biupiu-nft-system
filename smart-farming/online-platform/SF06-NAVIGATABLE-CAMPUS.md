# SF-06 — Biupiu Navigable Learning Campus

## Purpose
Create a navigable digital environment layered on top of the Biupiu Online OS. Learning spaces use visual reconstructions and interactive simulations to teach historical/agricultural systems while clearly distinguishing reconstruction from archaeological fact.

## Campus map
- Biupiu Welcome Plaza
- Research Library
- Digital Laboratory
- Farming Systems District
- Ancient Water & Agriculture District
- Materials & Biocomposites Lab
- Robotics & AI Lab
- Plant Intelligence Conservatory
- Academy / Course Centre
- Community Commons
- Genesis Gallery

## Interaction model
Walk/navigate -> enter facility -> inspect objects -> open research cards -> watch lesson -> launch Digital Lab experiment -> save progress -> return to OS.

## Device strategy
The first implementation should be browser/mobile-friendly 2D/2.5D. A later 3D version can support avatars and real-time multi-user interaction.
