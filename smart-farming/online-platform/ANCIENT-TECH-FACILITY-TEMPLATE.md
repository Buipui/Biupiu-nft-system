# Ancient Technology Learning Facility Template v0.1

Every historical-technology facility uses the same architecture:

## 1. Historical context
What is documented and what remains uncertain.

## 2. Digital reconstruction
Interactive environment based on documented geometry/materials where available.

## 3. Measurement layer
Variables that can be measured or simulated.

## 4. Research layer
Primary/secondary sources linked to the reconstruction.

## 5. Digital experiment
User-adjustable parameters and repeatable runs.

## 6. Modern engineering comparison
Compare the documented system with modern alternatives without assuming equivalence.

## 7. Biupiu hypothesis lab
A clearly marked area for speculative ideas that can be tested.

## 8. Course connection
Facility links to Academy lessons, quizzes and projects.

## 9. Community layer
Future users can visit together, discuss results and collaborate on experiments.
