## Archive policy
Keep:
1. editable source project
2. source media
3. approved renders
4. captions/subtitles
5. manifests
6. provenance/rights records
7. final approval record
8. release version history

Never overwrite the approved release candidate without incrementing its version.

## Release blockers
Publication remains blocked if:
- rights are unresolved
- required claim labels are missing
- human editorial approval is absent
- critical render errors remain
- provenance cannot be established
- required captions/accessibility checks fail

This is a release-candidate specification, not evidence that the episode has been published.
