## Editorial QA
### Story
- World introduction is immediately understandable.
- Scene order follows SF-22.
- Each department receives enough screen time to identify its purpose.
- Ending resolves back into the persistent Biupiu World.

### Visual
- Consistent materials, lighting and colour language.
- No accidental visual discontinuities between districts.
- Vehicle silhouettes remain consistent with their registered specifications.
- Branding is restrained and legible.

### Claims
- Required CONCEPT / RESEARCH CONCEPT / RESEARCH PROTOTYPE labels remain visible.
- No speculative capability is presented as a verified commercial product.
- Research reconstructions remain distinguishable from historical fact where relevant.

### Rights
- Every production asset has a provenance record.
- Third-party media is licensed or replaced.
- No prohibited game-derived asset is included.

### Accessibility
- Captions available.
- Critical information is not conveyed only through audio.
- Titles remain inside mobile-safe areas.
- Text remains readable at final delivery resolution.

PASS → CONDITIONAL PASS → REWORK → BLOCKED

A release candidate requires PASS or documented CONDITIONAL PASS with all blocking issues resolved.
