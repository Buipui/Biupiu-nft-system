# SF-14 — Nexus Mods Asset Audit

Date: 2026-09-18

## Result
Nexus Mods was checked for Assassin's Creed environment/vehicle/model resources. The current evidence does **not** establish a safe pool of Assassin's Creed game assets for commercial Biupiu use.

### Findings
- AnvilToolkit is available and supports many Anvil games, but its Nexus permissions expressly prohibit use of assets from the file and prohibit use in mods/files sold for money. citeturn0search0turn0search1
- Several Assassin's Creed Origins/Unity mods explicitly require permission before their assets may be used, and/or prohibit use in sold mods/files. Examples include Mesh Replacement, Hooded Spaniard, No Pouches, and several Unity mods. citeturn0search3turn0search5turn0search7turn0search12
- Some mod pages allow broader use of the author's own assets, but those permissions do not automatically grant rights to Ubisoft game assets or third-party assets used by the mod. citeturn0search6turn0search14

## Classification
**RESEARCH-ONLY:** Assassin's Creed extracted/proprietary assets and mods whose permissions are unclear or restrictive.

**POTENTIALLY USABLE:** An individual creator's original asset only where the exact page provides permission compatible with commercial redistribution, modification/conversion and Biupiu's intended use, and where all third-party dependencies are cleared.

**APPROVED FOR PRODUCTION:** None identified in this audit.

## Vehicles
No Assassin's Creed vehicle asset was identified in this audit with sufficiently clear rights for commercial Biupiu redistribution. Do not download/import a vehicle merely because Nexus provides a download button.

## Production route
Use Nexus pages for visual/technical research, then source production assets from original creators or repositories with explicit commercial licences. Original Biupiu vehicles/environments should be created where no suitable licensed asset exists.

## Repository rule
No Nexus download is to be imported into a Biupiu commercial build until the asset-lot licence register contains documentary permission and all dependencies are cleared.
