# SF-47 — Biupiu AeroBlade GT Visual Production Package

Status: EXECUTED — VISUAL PRODUCTION SPECIFICATION READY
Parent gate: SF-46
Project: BPU-AUTO-SPORT-001

## Objective
Convert the corrected AeroBlade GT architecture into an original visual showcase for Biupiu World, the Digital Lab, automotive research and product advertising.

## Design continuity
- Sleek sports-car silhouette with faceted-to-curved computational-geometry surfaces
- Stealth-inspired surface language without claims of invisibility or military performance
- Golden-mean proportion guidance for visual massing and feature spacing
- Biupiu resin architected/metasurface coating shown as a research concept
- HempCarbon and advanced composite material zones
- Integrated Biupiu AeroBrake
- Rear/mid turbocharged biodiesel-compatible engine with mechanical rear-wheel drive and electrical-generation support
- Individual high-output electric motors at each wheel; front hub/traction motors shown as torque-vectoring/AWD research
- Biupiu microturbine-generator modules supplying electrical power to the HV system

## Render sequence
1. Hero front three-quarter view — full vehicle and AeroBlade identity
2. Side profile — golden-mean construction overlay
3. Rear three-quarter view — rear/mid engine packaging and mechanical rear-drive emphasis
4. Powertrain cutaway — engine, rear transmission, HV bus and energy storage
5. Front wheel hub-motor detail — high-output electric motor and thermal-management concept
6. Rear wheel propulsion detail — mechanical drive plus electric motor assistance
7. Microturbine-generator detail — compact auxiliary electrical-generation module
8. Energy-flow visualisation — engine generation, microturbines, battery/HV bus and motors
9. AeroBrake deployment concept — aerodynamic surface movement, clearly labelled CONCEPT
10. Biupiu resin metasurface macro — architected micro/nano texture and coating layers
11. HempCarbon/composite material macro — material stack and manufacturing research callouts
12. Computational geometry overlay — surface topology, airflow study zones and controlled wake targets
13. Biupiu World showroom placement — automotive research district, Digital Lab and Academy links
