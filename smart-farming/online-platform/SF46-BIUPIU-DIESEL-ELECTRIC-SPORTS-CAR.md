## Core propulsion architecture
The AeroBlade GT is a parallel diesel-electric technology demonstrator. The rear/mid-mounted turbocharged biodiesel-compatible engine participates directly in propulsion by mechanically driving the rear wheels, while also supporting the electrical system through an electrical-generation path. Electric hub/traction motors provide additional wheel torque, regeneration and torque-vectoring capability.

- Rear/mid-mounted turbocharged biodiesel-compatible engine
- Mechanical rear-wheel drive through a suitable rear transaxle/transmission
- Engine-driven electrical-generation path for the high-voltage system
- High-voltage DC bus and energy-storage buffer
- Individual high-output electric traction motors at each wheel as the digital architecture target
- Rear wheels: mechanical engine drive plus individual rear electric motor assistance
- Front wheels: individual high-output electric hub/traction motors
- Front electric motors support torque vectoring and AWD research
- Regenerative braking
- Supervisory energy-management controller
- Thermal management for engine, generator, inverters, motors and battery
- Fault containment and HV isolation as mandatory physical-prototype gates

The engine therefore has two functions: direct mechanical propulsion of the rear wheels and electrical-energy support. The microturbines are an additional electrical-generation system; they do not replace the engine's mechanical rear-wheel drive.

## Microturbine system
A compact Biupiu microturbine-generator module is included as an auxiliary electrical-generation research system. Its design direction is informed by the historical Jaguar C-X75 turbine concept, while the Biupiu vehicle geometry, packaging, controls and product integration remain original.

Target showcase role:
- supplementary electrical generation
- sustained electrical support for the HV bus
- high-power energy-system research
- compact turbine-generator packaging
- thermal-management research
- visual demonstration of Bioblade microturbine technology

The turbine system is a concept/research subsystem until engineered, tested and validated.

## Golden-mean aesthetic system
