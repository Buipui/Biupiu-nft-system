## Architecture
The vehicle is a diesel-electric technology demonstrator built around a rear/mid-mounted turbocharged biodiesel-compatible engine-generator. The engine is not used as the primary wheel propulsion source.

- Rear/mid-mounted turbocharged biodiesel-compatible engine
- Engine mechanically coupled to an electrical generator
- High-voltage DC power bus
- Energy storage buffer
- Individual electric traction motors at each wheel
- Rear-wheel drive bias through independent rear wheel motors
- Front wheel motors available for torque-vectoring / all-wheel-drive research
- Regenerative braking
- Supervisory energy-management controller
- Thermal management for engine, generator, inverter and motors

This resolves the requested "engine not for propulsion" requirement: the combustion engine supplies electrical energy rather than mechanically driving the wheels. The rear wheels are driven by their electric motors. Any future mechanical engine-to-wheel variant is a separate engineering study.

## Microturbine system
A compact microturbine auxiliary-generation module is included as a research option, inspired by the general engineering direction of historical turbine-powered sports-car concepts such as the Jaguar C-X75, but with original Biupiu architecture and geometry.

Potential role:
- supplementary electrical generation
- high-power transient support
- thermal/energy research
- experimental turbine-generator integration

It is not represented as validated or production-ready.

## Aerodynamics
Computational-geometry design targets:
- slender low-drag silhouette
- long controlled body arcs
- faceted-to-curved stealth-inspired surface transitions
- integrated wheel-arch airflow management
- underbody flow channels
- controlled rear wake
- active/aerodynamic research surfaces
- Biupiu AeroBrake integration
- cooling apertures shaped as part of the surface topology

The AeroBrake is a research/concept aerodynamic device. No braking or downforce performance is claimed.

## Golden-mean aesthetic system
The computational geometry engine should use golden-ratio relationships as an aesthetic proportion guide for:
- wheelbase-to-body-length relationships
- cabin and greenhouse placement
- nose-to-cabin-to-tail visual mass
- wheel diameter and body feature spacing
- side-surface feature intervals
- cockpit and glazing proportions

Golden-ratio construction is an aesthetic design method, not a guarantee of aerodynamic or structural performance.

## Meta-surface coating concept
Biupiu resin research is assigned to an architected surface-coating study:
- resin matrix concept
- controlled micro/nano surface texture
- computationally generated repeating/graded geometry
- optical/thermal/wetting response research
- advanced-material surface zones
- PBR visualization for digital world

The coating is a research concept until formulation, durability, environmental, manufacturing and performance testing are completed.
