## Objective
Define an original, rights-cleared concept-render workflow for the Biupiu AeroBlade GT and prepare assets for later video integration and Biupiu World showroom placement.

## Locked design continuity
- Sleek sports-car silhouette using computational-geometry proportions and faceted-to-curved transitions.
- Golden-mean proportions used as an aesthetic guide only; no structural or aerodynamic performance is implied.
- Biupiu resin architected/metasurface coating remains a research concept pending measured validation.
- HempCarbon and advanced-composite zones remain material research concepts.
- Integrated AeroBrake is a visual/mechanical research concept pending engineering validation.
- Rear/mid-mounted turbocharged biodiesel-compatible engine mechanically drives the rear wheels through a suitable transmission/transaxle and can support electrical generation.
- Individual high-output electric traction/hub motors are allocated to each wheel in the digital architecture; rear electric assistance supplements mechanical rear drive, while front motors support torque-vectoring/AWD research.
- Biupiu microturbine-generator modules provide auxiliary electrical generation to the high-voltage system; they are not represented as direct mechanical wheel drives.
