# Biupiu OS Online Platform — Subscription Architecture v0.1

## Purpose
Provide secure online access to the Biupiu Farming System OS, research library, Digital Laboratory, Intelligence Hub assistance and community features while keeping account permissions, research access and experimental data clearly separated.

## Initial service tiers
### Explorer
- Read-only public/approved research library
- Limited Digital Laboratory experiments
- Basic AI research assistant
- Public tutorials and how-to content

### Researcher
- Expanded research library
- Personal experiment workspace
- Saved searches and datasets
- AI-assisted experiment design
- Plant profile and sensor simulation tools

### Professional
- Advanced plant/crop profiles
- Larger experiment quotas
- Connected-device dashboards
- Team workspaces
- Advanced analytics and reporting

### Institutional
- Multi-user organisation workspace
- Role-based permissions
- Private research areas
- Shared datasets and experiment governance
- Administration and audit controls

Subscription features should be configurable rather than hard-coded so Biupiu can change plans without rewriting the OS.

## Core online services
- Identity/account service
- Subscription/entitlement service
- Research Library search
- Digital Laboratory
- Intelligence Hub AI assistant
- Plant Intelligence catalogue
- Experiment storage
- Tutorial/video library
- Community service
- Future spatial/Avatar layer

## Data separation
Customer private experiments must remain distinct from Biupiu research datasets unless the user explicitly enables sharing. Shared datasets require appropriate governance, provenance and privacy controls.

## Offline-first principle
The local Farming OS must continue monitoring installed devices when internet access is unavailable. Subscription status must not create an unsafe physical-device state.
