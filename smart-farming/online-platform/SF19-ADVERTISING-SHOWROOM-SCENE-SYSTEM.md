## Reusable advertising frame
Every scene follows:
BRAND HEADER
→ HERO PRODUCT/VEHICLE
→ DEPARTMENT IDENTIFIER
→ SHORT VERIFIED VALUE STATEMENT
→ MEDIA/3D DEMO
→ RESEARCH / ACADEMY / DIGITAL LAB
→ ENQUIRY / CATALOGUE

## Content rule
Copy must distinguish:
- PRODUCT
- PROTOTYPE
- CONCEPT
- RESEARCH
- SIMULATION

Unverified performance or commercial-availability claims are not permitted.

## Asset slots
Each scene supports:
- hero 3D asset
- secondary products
- video
- still images
- logo
- department mark
- specifications
- QR/deep-link equivalent
- research links
- course links
- marketplace link
