## Required asset record
Each render must contain:
- asset_id
- project_id: BPU-AUTO-SPORT-001
- scene_id
- design_pass
- source_type
- provenance
- rights_status
- claim_class
- review_status
- intended_outputs

## Production source rule
AeroBlade GT production geometry must be original Biupiu work or explicitly licensed material whose licence permits the intended commercial use, modification and redistribution. Game/mod assets remain reference-only unless exact rights are documented.

## Visual review
Check:
- established stealth-geometric Biupiu language
- faceted-to-curved continuity
- golden-mean proportion guidance used as an aesthetic study only
- AeroBrake representation
- HempCarbon/composite material language
- resin metasurface research treatment
- coherent wheel/motor packaging
- coherent rear mechanical drivetrain
- coherent microturbine-to-HV architecture
- premium biotech × regenerative × advanced-materials identity

## Claim review
Permitted labels include CONCEPT, RESEARCH CONCEPT and DESIGN STUDY. Do not state or imply validated stealth, invisibility, radar-proof performance, certified aerodynamics, measured braking/performance, road legality, production readiness or commercial availability.

## Video integration
Approved stills become source assets for SF-22/SF-44/SF-47 production. Planned outputs: 16:9 master, 9:16 vertical, 4:5 campaign cut, 30-second trailer, 15-second teaser and Biupiu World showroom sequence.
