# Course 04 — AI-Assisted Farming

## Lessons
1. What AI can and cannot infer from farm data
2. Building a useful dataset
3. Writing testable hypotheses
4. Using the Biupiu Intelligence Assistant
5. Designing controlled comparisons
6. Detecting poor or missing measurements
7. Turning observations into recommendations
8. Reporting and versioning results

## Capstone
Create an AI-assisted growth-cycle experiment and submit the hypothesis, variables, dataset, recommendation and measured outcome.
