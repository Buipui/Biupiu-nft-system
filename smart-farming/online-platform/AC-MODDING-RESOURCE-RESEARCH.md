# Assassin's Creed Modding Resource Research — Rights-Gated

## Research findings
Assassin's Creed modding communities provide useful references for studying environment pipelines and Anvil-engine modding. Nexus Mods currently lists AnvilToolkit for Assassin's Creed Origins; its page states that assets from that toolkit may not be used and that the toolkit itself may not be redistributed without permission. citeturn0search2

Nexus Mods' current file guidelines state that copyrighted game files/images are prohibited unless proper permission has been granted, and that credit alone does not replace permission. citeturn0search0turn0search14

Ubisoft's public video policy also states that game assets should not be taken from its games and distributed separately. citeturn0search7

## Approved use for Biupiu
Use modding sites as research references for:
- environment scale and composition ideas;
- historical-game environment workflows;
- modding/toolchain research;
- references to publicly documented techniques;
- discovery of assets only where the licence explicitly permits Biupiu's intended use.

## Not approved
Do not copy, scrape, extract, repackage or commercially distribute Assassin's Creed character models, game environments, textures, animations, audio or other proprietary game assets.

## Safe production alternative
Recreate Biupiu environments from independent historical research using original geometry, original textures/materials and properly licensed/public-domain references. The visual target can be historically rich without making the Biupiu World dependent on Ubisoft assets.
