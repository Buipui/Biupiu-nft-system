# Waru Waru Learning Facility — Digital Environment Specification

## Educational purpose
Teach the structure and agricultural logic of raised-bed / sunken-channel systems commonly described as waru waru in the Andes, using an interactive reconstruction.

## Environment
A landscape scene contains:
- raised cultivation beds;
- water channels between beds;
- surrounding terrain;
- crop plots;
- water-flow visualisation;
- measurement stations;
- interpretation/learning pavilion.

## Interactive stations
1. **Landscape overview** — explains the reconstructed system.
2. **Water station** — simulate water movement through channels.
3. **Thermal station** — explore measured/assumed temperature differences through a simulation.
4. **Crop station** — select a hypothetical crop profile and run a digital experiment.
5. **Research station** — open approved Biupiu research sources.
6. **Modern comparison** — compare the digital reconstruction with a conventional raised-bed scenario.

## Evidence handling
The environment must label archaeological/historical information, modern engineering interpretation, simulation assumptions and Biupiu hypotheses separately. It should not present speculative explanations as historical fact.

## Learning activity
Learner changes channel depth/bed geometry/water input in the Digital Laboratory and observes simulated outputs. Results are labelled simulation results.
