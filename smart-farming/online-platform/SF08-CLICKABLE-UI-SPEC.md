# SF-08 — Clickable Biupiu Campus UI

## Prototype goal
Provide a coherent mobile/desktop interaction shell for the Biupiu digital campus without requiring a 3D engine.

## Primary navigation
Home | Campus | Research | Lab | Plants | Academy | AI | Community | Profile

## Campus screen
District cards -> facility cards -> station cards.

## Facility screen
Facility overview, evidence notice, available lessons, experiments and interactive stations.

## Waru Waru interaction
Tap a station -> open station panel -> read/inspect -> Launch Lab / Open Research / Continue Lesson.

## Persistent context
When a user launches the Digital Laboratory from a facility, preserve facility_id, station_id, plant_id (if selected), lesson_id (if selected) and experiment template.

## Progress
Record facility visits, completed lessons, completed experiments and saved results in the user's private progress record.
