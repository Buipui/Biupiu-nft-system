# Biupiu Course Creator Guide v0.1

## Standard course structure
1. Promise and learning outcomes
2. Short introductory video
3. Concept lesson
4. Demonstration
5. Digital Laboratory activity
6. Downloadable workbook/checklist
7. Knowledge check
8. Practical assignment
9. AI-assisted reflection
10. Completion/certificate criteria

## Commercial packaging
Courses may be sold individually, bundled into learning paths, included in selected subscriptions or offered to institutions.

## Content governance
Course claims must distinguish established information, Biupiu research, experimental results and hypotheses. Update course versions when underlying research or software changes.

## Suggested learning paths
**Starter:** Course 01 + 02
**Maker:** 02 + 03 + 09
**AI Researcher:** 04 + 08 + 05
**Regenerative Technology:** 02 + 06 + 07
**Product Developer:** 03 + 07 + 10

## Completion records
Store course version, learner progress, assessment results and certificate identifier. Do not represent completion as professional licensing or agricultural certification unless a separately recognised credential is actually issued.
