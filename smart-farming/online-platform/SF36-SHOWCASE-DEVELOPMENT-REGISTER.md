## Showcase rule
Each development maps to development_id, department_id, showcase_scene, status, claim_class, research links, Digital Lab links, Academy links and catalogue/marketplace links where applicable.

Claim classes: CONCEPT, RESEARCH, PROTOTYPE, DEMONSTRATOR, AVAILABLE_PRODUCT only when verified.

The showcase must distinguish current research from completed commercial products.
