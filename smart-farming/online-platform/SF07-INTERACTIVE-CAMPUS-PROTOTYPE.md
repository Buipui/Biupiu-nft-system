# SF-07 — Interactive Biupiu Campus Prototype

## Goal
Turn the SF-06 architecture into a first functional browser/mobile prototype using a lightweight 2D/2.5D interface.

## Prototype navigation
HOME -> CAMPUS MAP -> DISTRICT -> FACILITY -> STATION -> CONTENT -> DIGITAL LAB

## Waru Waru prototype scene
The scene contains:
- raised cultivation beds;
- water channels;
- crop plots;
- terrain/background layer;
- water-flow visualisation toggle;
- six interactive stations;
- facility information panel;
- Launch Experiment action.

## Station IDs
WW-01 Overview
WW-02 Water Flow
WW-03 Thermal Simulation
WW-04 Crop Experiment
WW-05 Research Sources
WW-06 Modern Comparison

## Accessibility
All important information must also be available as ordinary text panels. Navigation must work without 3D graphics, audio or motion effects.

## Future compatibility
Facility and station IDs are stable identifiers that can later be rendered by a 3D/immersive client without changing the underlying research content.
