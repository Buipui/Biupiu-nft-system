# SF-24 — Five-Scene Biupiu World Visual Storyboard

Date: 2026-09-18

## Purpose
Create the first cohesive visual storyboard for the five persistent Biupiu World showcase locations.

## Scene sequence
1. AIR-01 — Advanced Mobility Hangar
2. MAR-01 — Marine Research Port
3. AGR-01 — Regenerative Farm Gateway
4. CONS-01 — Conservation Expedition Base
5. AI-01 — Intelligence Hub Showroom

## Visual continuity
All five scenes share:
- Biupiu master visual identity
- consistent materials and lighting language
- original vehicle concepts
- premium architectural CGI
- connected landscape/world design
- restrained advertising treatment

## Production status
The five-scene sheet is a visual storyboard reference. Individual scene renders should become separate production shots in the next render pass.

## Asset rule
No proprietary GTA V, Forza, Assassin's Creed, Battlefield, Hell Let Loose, Arma or other game assets are part of the production geometry.

## Future replacement
As Biupiu CAD, product designs, videos and approved 3D assets become available, each concept vehicle can be replaced without changing the scene IDs or episode structure.
