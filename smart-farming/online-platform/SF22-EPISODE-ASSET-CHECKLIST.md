# SF-22 Asset Checklist

## Required
- Biupiu logo/brand assets
- five department vehicle concept assets
- five showcase environment scenes
- department identity graphics
- verified descriptive copy
- original/licensed audio
- camera paths
- lighting presets
- title/end-card graphics

## Future replacement
Concept renders can later be replaced by:
- final 3D models
- product photography
- product videos
- CAD-derived renders
- licensed third-party components
- user-supplied original footage

The shot structure remains unchanged.

## Rights gate
Every final episode asset must have:
- provenance
- rights status
- source
- creator
- permitted use
- claim status

Research-only material remains excluded from production advertising.
