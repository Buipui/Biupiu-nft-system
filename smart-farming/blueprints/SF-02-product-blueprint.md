# SF-02 Premium Product Blueprint v0.1

## Form factor
Freestanding or wall-mounted field console with a portrait/landscape-capable touchscreen, sealed electronics compartment and modular external sensor harness.

## Proposed stack
[TOP] status/light bar
[FRONT] capacitive touchscreen + minimal physical emergency/service controls
[CORE] Raspberry Pi + power management + storage
[LOWER] removable Arduino/edge-controller module
[REAR] protected sensor/power/service ports
[EXTERNAL] replaceable sensor nodes

## Housing material strategy
Primary candidate: Biupiu hemp/natural-fibre composite shell.
Secondary candidates: recycled/natural-fibre hybrid composite and mechanically reinforced bio-composite panels.

The final material is selected through measured mechanical, water, UV, thermal and fire-performance testing—not by assumption from fibre content alone.

## Manufacturing path
CAD -> material coupons -> enclosure prototype -> environmental testing -> electronics integration -> field pilot -> design freeze -> production drawing.

## Premium details
- Flush touchscreen bezel.
- Hidden/controlled fasteners.
- Replaceable service panels.
- Modular sensor ports.
- Embossed/laser-marked Biupiu identity.
- Neutral premium finish suitable for agricultural, research and institutional deployments.
