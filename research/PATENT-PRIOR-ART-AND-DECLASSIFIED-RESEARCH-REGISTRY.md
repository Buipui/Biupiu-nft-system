# Biupiu Patent Prior-Art & Declassified Research Registry

**Record:** BPU-RES-PAT-001  
**Status:** Active cross-reference framework  
**Scope:** Personal R&D / NFT research portfolio

## Purpose

This registry establishes a controlled prior-art and patent cross-reference layer for Biupiu research and development. It incorporates the previously requested patent-registry searches and adds a dedicated workflow for historically declassified or released government patent/technical material where such records are legally and publicly accessible.

The registry is intended to help Biupiu:

- identify existing disclosures before developing new concepts;
- distinguish genuinely novel combinations from known techniques;
- identify expired, abandoned, active and pending patent families;
- trace government-sponsored research disclosures;
- identify declassified historical technologies that may be relevant to engineering research;
- compare patent claims with Biupiu concepts;
- generate safer R&D directions that avoid simply reproducing known patented claims;
- document provenance for computational artwork and NFT research records.

**Important:** A patent is evidence that an invention was disclosed/claimed, not proof that every technical assertion in it works. Patent status and freedom-to-operate conclusions require professional patent/legal review.

## Required Search Sources

### Patent / Prior-Art Sources

1. **Google Patents** — broad patent-family discovery and full-text claim/description searching.
2. **USPTO Patent Center / official USPTO records** — authoritative U.S. prosecution and status records where available.
3. **WIPO PATENTSCOPE** — international/PCT patent searching.
4. **EPO Espacenet** — international patent-family and prior-art searching.
5. **The Lens** — patent and scholarly cross-reference.

### Government / Declassified Research Sources

Future searches should also check appropriate official repositories for publicly released/declassified technical material, including:

- U.S. National Archives and Records Administration (NARA)
- CIA Reading Room where technically relevant and lawfully public
- U.S. Department of Defense / Defense Technical Information Center (DTIC) public records where available
- NASA NTRS and TechPort
- DOE / OSTI technical records
- NSA declassified records where relevant and publicly available
- National laboratories and official government technical repositories
- South African National Archives / government repositories where relevant and accessible

Declassified material must be treated according to its evidence level and historical context. Declassification does not establish scientific validity.

## Declassified Patent / Technology Rule

When a historical or declassified record is found, the registry must distinguish:

1. **Declassified historical document** — document status only.
2. **Technical disclosure** — what the document actually describes.
3. **Demonstrated technology** — independently documented experimental/engineering evidence.
4. **Patent claim** — legal claim scope, which is separate from technical proof.
5. **Biupiu hypothesis** — a new interpretation or proposed development.

No declassified document should be treated as proof of extraordinary performance merely because it was classified, declassified, government-associated or patented.

## Patentability / IP Boundary

This registry supports research and prior-art awareness. It is **not** a legal opinion, patentability opinion, freedom-to-operate opinion or substitute for a registered patent attorney/agent. Before public disclosure of a potentially patentable Biupiu invention, preserve the invention record and obtain appropriate professional IP advice.
