# BIUPIU-VOC — Vibes of Cosmos Atlas Integration

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 15 September 2026  
**Status:** Executed research-index integration

## Purpose

Integrate Vibes of Cosmos (VOC) maps and related videos into the Biupiu Ancient Landscape Atlas as a **speculative cartographic layer**. VOC material is not used as the physical/geodetic baseline and is not treated as archaeological proof.

## Source families

- World Map / Plasma Moon map family
- Physical World Map
- Pangaia Selenografic Map
- Extended World Map
- World Map 2025
- VOC YouTube/video presentations and related public source material

The published VOC maps include conventional geographic features together with alternative or hypothetical layers such as Pangaia, hidden lands, Middle Earth/Narnia, Antarctica/ice-wall representations and lands beyond Antarctica. These are preserved as source claims and separated from established geography. See the published map descriptions and version records in the repository source index.

## Atlas layer architecture

### BASE-0 — Physical Earth
Modern geodesy, satellite imagery, authoritative bathymetry, geology and national mapping datasets.

### BASE-1 — Historical Earth
Historical maps, cadastral surveys, exploration charts and documented historical geography.

### BASE-2 — Palaeogeography
Palaeocoastlines, sea-level change, palaeorivers, glacial extent, submerged shelves and ancient environmental reconstruction.

### BASE-3 — Archaeological Earth
Settlements, monuments, roads, mines, agricultural systems, ports, temples, caves and documented archaeological landscapes.

### BASE-4 — Cultural/Traditional Geography
Mythological, sacred and traditional geographical concepts, explicitly labelled as cultural sources.

### BASE-5 — BIUPIU-VOC
VOC alternative-cartography claims, preserved by map version and claim ID.

## Map-comparison workflow

`VOC source map → provenance/version capture → feature extraction → claim-to-coordinate conversion → projection/georeferencing → modern geodetic comparison → bathymetry/geology → palaeogeography → archaeology → statistical/physical test → evidence update`

No speculative feature is promoted to established geography solely because it appears on a map.

## Antarctica / hidden-land protocol

For any proposed land beyond or surrounding Antarctica, test:

- satellite and radar observations
- ice thickness
- subglacial topography
- gravity and magnetic datasets
- bathymetry
- continental/oceanic crust evidence
- palaeoclimate and palaeo-coastline models
- historical exploration and mapping
- archaeological evidence where applicable.

## Pangaia protocol

Keep the following concepts separate:

- geological Pangaea reconstruction
- VOC Pangaia model
- mythological/cultural lost-land traditions
- submerged Pleistocene landscapes
- Atlantis/Mu/Lemuria/Hyperborea hypotheses.

Similarity in map geometry is not evidence of common origin. Test chronology, geography, source genealogy and physical feasibility.

## Sepehr / Atlantean Gardens / Divergent cross-links

## Evidence classification

- **A:** directly demonstrated.
- **B:** strongly supported by independent reliable evidence.
- **C:** plausible/incomplete.
- **D:** testable hypothesis.
- **E:** unsupported or contradicted by current evidence.
- **M:** mythological/cultural source.

VOC cartographic claims currently enter primarily as **D**, **E**, or **M** depending on the individual claim. Conventional geographic features shown on VOC maps are not automatically validated by their appearance there; they are checked against authoritative datasets.

## Copyright / IP

VOC maps are third-party works and are identified as all-rights-reserved in their published listings. Biupiu should preserve metadata, citations and analytical descriptions rather than reproducing protected map artwork in NFT assets without permission. Biupiu-generated GIS, statistical and computational outputs should be independently documented and licensed.

## Atlas objective

The purpose of this integration is not to establish a particular cosmology. It is to turn alternative maps into **explicit geographic predictions** that can be compared with physical Earth data, historical cartography, archaeology and palaeogeography.

**Biupiu principle:** Preserve the hypothesis. Separate the evidence. Test the prediction.
