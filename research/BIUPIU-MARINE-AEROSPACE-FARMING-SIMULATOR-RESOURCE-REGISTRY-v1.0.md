## Marine
Research candidates: Naval Group LOTUSim/lxdyn, Gazebo maritime, MSS, PythonVehicleSimulator, MARUSim, Stonefish ROS2, Aeolus-Ocean, openMSN, PySeidon and marine co-simulation/FMUs. These cover surface/underwater dynamics, waves, propulsion, GNC, sensors, COLREG scenarios, distributed simulation and Digital Twin workflows.

## Aerospace / Space
Research candidates: JSBSim, AeroSim, Basilisk, OpenRocket ecosystem, PX4/FlightGear bridges and aerospace Digital Twin/airport-twin patterns. These cover aircraft 6-DOF/flight dynamics, spacecraft attitude/orbit simulation, FMU/co-simulation, Unreal/Omniverse rendering, autopilot interfaces and mission/telemetry modelling.

## Farming
Research candidates: farm-twin, Farm-Twin, AgriTwin, Agri-twin, KrishiDisha/WOFOST-AquaCrop-DSSAT patterns, Agrar-Simulator and ROS/Gazebo agricultural robotics patterns. These cover soil/weather/crop state, irrigation, nutrient/yield modelling, farm operations, robotics, feedback and explainable advisory systems.
