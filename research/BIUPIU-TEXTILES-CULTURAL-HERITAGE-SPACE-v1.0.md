# Biupiu TEXTILES v1.0

**Status:** Architecture specification
**Date:** 18 September 2026

## Purpose

A culturally contextualised textile research space with Southern African, West African, North African/Mediterranean, South Asian, East Asian and Global Fibre Innovation environments. It covers documented fibres, weaving, dyeing, cordage, basketry, silk, cotton, natural pigments, pattern systems, historical trade and modern fibre/composite R&D. Historical material is evidence-classified and modern performance claims require testing. Access is TEXTILES entitlement only.

## Environment rule

Each environment combines documented cultural context with a specialist workspace. Modern experiments are recorded separately from historical reconstruction. Cross-department resources require explicit SHARED classification.
