# Biupiu World — New Age Theory / Lost Civilisations & Inner-Earth Research Space v1.0

**Date:** 18 September 2026  
**Status:** World architecture + controlled speculative research specification  
**Evidence policy:** Speculative narratives are research objects, not established historical facts.

## Purpose

The **NEW AGE THEORY** space is a dedicated Biupiu World environment for studying lost-civilisation narratives, lost lands, submerged landscapes, inner-Earth traditions, esoteric geography, alternative chronology and related modern claims.

The space is deliberately separated from the historically established civilisation layer. It allows unusual ideas to be explored without silently promoting them to archaeological or scientific fact.

## Reality-control environments

The New Age Theory space also contains a **REAL-WORLD LOST-LAND CONTROL LIBRARY**. These are not speculative continents; they are documented palaeolandscapes that help test how real land can be submerged or transformed.

### CTRL-DOG — Doggerland
Submerged prehistoric landscape in the North Sea. Use as a control case for:
- sea-level rise
- palaeochannels
- submerged archaeology
- Mesolithic occupation
- geophysical reconstruction

### CTRL-SUN — Sundaland
Formerly exposed Southeast Asian lowland landscape during lower sea levels. Use for:
- palaeogeography
- river systems
- hominin dispersal
- submerged archaeology
- sea-level reconstruction

### CTRL-BER — Beringia
Ice-age land connection/refugial landscape between Siberia and North America. Use for:
- migration
- palaeoclimate
- human occupation
- land-bridge submergence

### CTRL-SAH — Sahul Shelf
Submerged portions of the ancient Australia–New Guinea continental landmass. Use for:
- human migration
- marine geophysics
- submerged cultural landscapes
- sea-level change

### CTRL-ZEA — Te Riu-a-Māui / Zealandia
A genuinely documented mostly submerged continent. Use as a geological control case for:
- crustal structure
- plate tectonics
- continental reconstruction
- bathymetry
- palaeogeography

**Important:** These control environments demonstrate that submerged landmasses are real; they do not validate Atlantis, Mu, Hyperborea, Tartaria or Hollow Earth claims.

## Evidence-state model

Every object in the New Age Theory space receives one or more controlled states:

- **DOCUMENTED:** supported by strong primary or archaeological/historical evidence.
- **HISTORICAL-INTERPRETATION:** established source exists, but location/meaning remains debated.
- **RECONSTRUCTED:** visual or spatial model derived from evidence.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** proposed explanation that can be investigated.
- **LEGEND:** traditional narrative or folklore.
- **SPECULATIVE:** modern extraordinary claim without sufficient independent evidence.
- **FICTIONAL/CREATIVE:** explicitly invented world-building material.

No state may silently promote a lower-evidence claim to DOCUMENTED.

## Claim-testing architecture

All New Age Theory objects enter the same Biupiu R&D pipeline:

SOURCE → CLAIM → SOURCE PROVENANCE → HISTORICAL CONTEXT → COUNTER-EVIDENCE → HYPOTHESIS → TESTABLE PREDICTION → MODEL → SIMULATION / ARCHIVAL TEST → EXPERIMENT / FIELD RESEARCH → DATASET → RESULT → REPLICATION → STATUS

Negative and inconclusive results remain permanently searchable.

## World-building rules

1. New Age Theory is an explicit speculative environment, not the default historical map.
2. Atlantis, Mu, Hyperborea, Tartaria and Hollow Earth receive dedicated environments.
3. A real historical civilisation may be linked as a comparison without being absorbed into a speculative claim.
4. Cultural and religious traditions are represented in their own historical context.
5. Modern internet theories are traceable to their source lineage.
6. Creative 3D scenes are labelled as reconstructions or fictional interpretations.
7. AI retrieval must preserve evidence state and source provenance.
8. Department subscriber controls remain active inside every New Age Theory environment.
9. Safety and ethics controls apply to physical experiments; hazardous or unverified procedures are not presented as validated engineering.
10. The system must preserve uncertainty instead of forcing binary true/false outputs where evidence is genuinely incomplete.

- Hollow Earth has a dedicated environment and physics-reality-check module.
