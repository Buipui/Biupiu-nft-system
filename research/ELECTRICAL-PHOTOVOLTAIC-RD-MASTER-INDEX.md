## Experimental Discipline

Every proposed advantage must be compared against a conventional control geometry. Record:

- geometry and dimensions
- material properties
- boundary conditions
- incident power/irradiance
- electrical load
- temperature
- efficiency
- losses
- uncertainty
- simulation software and mesh settings
- experimental instrumentation
- reproducibility

No claim of anomalous, over-unity or self-sustaining energy is accepted without independently reproducible measurements and energy-accounting controls.
