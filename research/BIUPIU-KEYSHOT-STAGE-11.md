# Biupiu KeyShot Stage 11 — Material & Environment Provenance

## Gate objective
Establish a legal/provenance-controlled library architecture for Biupiu-specific KeyShot materials and environments without bundling proprietary KeyShot content.

## Material families
Automotive paint/composites; marine coatings; aerospace/eVTOL finishes; microturbine metal/thermal surfaces; hemp fibre composites; bio-resins/adhesives; textiles/fibres; ceramics; glass; packaging/product materials.

## Provenance rule
Every library record carries source, license, redistribution/modification permissions and provenance status. External assets remain references unless their redistribution rights are explicitly verified.

## Environment layer
Standardized profiles cover neutral studio, automotive, marine, aerospace, engineering, product-hero and showreel turntable use.

## Integration
Material metadata feeds render-job contracts and output provenance. The registry is renderer-neutral and can be consumed by licensed KeyShot installations or future render backends.

## Exclusions
No KeyShot binaries, activation material, proprietary libraries, cracks, license bypasses or unverified third-party assets are included.
