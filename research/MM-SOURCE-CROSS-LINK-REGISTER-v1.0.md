## Purpose

This register connects the Biupiu metamaterials/metasurfaces branch to four external evidence families requested for ongoing research: ResearchGate scholarly discovery, Emerald Insight engineering literature, NASA technical programmes/patents, and publicly released/declassified government records including the CIA Reading Room. Patent databases are treated as a separate prior-art layer.

The source network is a discovery and validation framework, not a claim that all sources have equal evidentiary status.

## 8. Evidence and IP Boundary

A peer-reviewed paper, ResearchGate record, Emerald article, NASA project, patent, or declassified document answers a different question. The repository must preserve those distinctions:

- scholarly literature → technical knowledge and methods;
- NASA/government record → documented programme or technology activity;
- patent → disclosed/claimed invention and prior art;
- declassified record → historical technical source;
- simulation → computational result;
- experiment → measured result;
- Biupiu concept → proposed development requiring its own validation.

No source in this register should be used to imply that a proposed Biupiu metamaterial design is proven, novel, patentable, or commercially superior without the corresponding technical or legal evidence.
