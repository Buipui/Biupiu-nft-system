# Biupiu Vehicle Bio-Composite Engineering Gate 01 v1.0

**Date:** 20 September 2026  
**Scope:** Personal R&D repository — hypercar and expedition/overlander case studies  
**Status:** IMPLEMENTED AT DOCUMENTATION LEVEL — ENGINEERING VALIDATION OPEN

## Gate objective
Convert the supplied vehicle concepts into a traceable, evidence-controlled engineering queue without treating user-supplied dimensions, ratings, packaging claims, or simulator verdicts as verified facts.

## Exterminate reconciliation
- Duplicate and overlapping vehicle concepts consolidated into one controlled gate record.
- Hypotheses retained; unsupported guarantees removed from acceptance language.
- No structural, powertrain, fuel-system, composite, or torque-vectoring claim is marked verified without primary documentation, calculations, test data, or specialist review.
- No prior research is deleted; superseded concepts remain traceable through this gate.

## Controlled workstreams
1. **Hypercar:** Audi 4.2 V8 TDI/FSI candidate review; Factory Five 818, Boxster, SLC, R8, Noble and GTM donor comparison; dual inboard axial-flux front drive; mass budget below 1,000 kg as a target only.
2. **Overlander:** OM606, Cummins 6BT, Iveco Daily 4x4, Fuso FG and Unimog platforms; HVH250 P2 integration; kinetic/three-point habitation isolation; battery and thermal architecture.
3. **Materials:** flax/carbon hybrid laminates, bio-resin toughness, inter-ply veils, impact and fatigue performance, environmental durability and repairability.
4. **Safety and legality:** chassis torsional stiffness, crash load paths, steering/suspension pickup integrity, braking, thermal protection, fuel compatibility, emissions and roadworthiness.

## Evidence gates
- **E01 Source verification:** OEM datasheets, service manuals, peer-reviewed studies and authoritative supplier documentation.
- **E02 Geometry:** measured CAD/package envelope, driveline angles, shaft/CV/joint loads and clearance stack-up.
- **E03 Mass and balance:** complete bill of mass, axle loads, CG, payload and thermal-system mass.
- **E04 Physics:** first-order calculations followed by FEA/multibody/thermal analysis where available.
- **E05 Integration:** control, inverter, battery, clutch, cooling, wiring and failure-mode analysis.
- **E06 Validation:** test plan, instrumentation, repeatability, safety review and raw-data retention.
- **E07 Promotion:** only evidence-backed results may be promoted to verified engineering conclusions or IP records.
