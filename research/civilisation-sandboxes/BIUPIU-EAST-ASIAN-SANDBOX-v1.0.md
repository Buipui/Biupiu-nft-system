# Biupiu East Asian Cultural Sandbox v1.0

**Environment ID:** CIV-EAST-ASIAN
**Model:** Biupiu Main Hub / Sandbox
**Departments:** AGRI, METALLURGY, TEXTILES, MATERIALS
**Access:** Controlled by department entitlement and environment permissions

## Purpose

A dedicated cultural sandbox covering documented agricultural systems, silk and fibre technologies, weaving, ceramics/kilns, porcelain, glazes, lacquer, mineral materials and historical metalworking. It includes Heritage Library, Farming Environment, Metalworking Environment, Textile Environment, Materials Environment and Reconstruction Lab. Individual cultures and periods remain separately tagged.

## Sandbox navigation

`MAIN HUB → EAST ASIAN SANDBOX → CULTURAL HUB → DEPARTMENT ENVIRONMENT → WORKSHOP/LAB → PROJECT → RESEARCH OBJECT`

## Core zones

1. **Heritage Library** — sources, archaeology/history records, artefacts, terminology and provenance.
2. **World/Regional Map** — locations, routes, landscapes and historical context.
3. **Farming Environment** — only where the relevant department is enabled.
4. **Metalworking Environment** — only where the relevant department is enabled.
5. **Textile Environment** — only where the relevant department is enabled.
6. **Materials Environment** — only where the relevant department is enabled.
7. **Reconstruction Lab** — computational and experimental reconstruction, separated from historical evidence.
8. **Modern Innovation Lab** — controlled modern R&D inspired by documented material or process knowledge.

## Data rule

Historical records, reconstructions and modern experiments are separate object classes. Every claim receives an evidence state: DOCUMENTED, RECONSTRUCTED, EXPERIMENTAL, HYPOTHESIS or SPECULATIVE.

## Access rule

Department entitlements remain authoritative. Entering a civilisation sandbox does not grant access to every department. Search, AI retrieval, projects, datasets, exports and recommendations inherit both the subscriber's department entitlement and the selected environment scope.

## Cultural integrity

The sandbox is not a claim that all traditions in the region formed one civilisation. Culture, site, language/community where appropriate, date/period and source provenance are explicit metadata fields.
