# Biupiu Original Engineering Video Series — Episode 01 Production Package

**Episode ID:** BIU-VID-S1-E01  
**Title:** From Video Claim to Engineering Test  
**Status:** PRE-PRODUCTION — original Biupiu production framework  
**Date:** 17 September 2026

## Purpose

Create an original Biupiu video using the Biupiu research model to demonstrate how a video claim is converted into a testable engineering question.

## Editorial promise

The episode distinguishes documented facts, supported engineering principles, Biupiu hypotheses, simulations and physical results. No simulation, concept render or third-party video is presented as a completed prototype or measured performance.

## Proposed runtime

8–12 minutes, expandable into short clips.

## Scene plan

1. **Opening (0:00–0:45):** Biupiu identity; engineering curiosity; statement that claims require testing.
2. **Source intake (0:45–2:00):** Show a fictional/example source card and explain creator attribution, URL, date, licence and claim extraction.
3. **Evidence ladder (2:00–3:15):** Documented, supported, experimental, hypothesis, speculative and falsification categories.
4. **Claim decomposition (3:15–4:30):** Convert one claim into mechanism, inputs, outputs, controls and failure conditions.
5. **Research model (4:30–6:00):** Source → CODEX → AAT/ALA/GEO → IP/prior art → simulation → prototype → measurement → review.
6. **Prototype gate (6:00–7:30):** Safety, instrumentation, calibration, uncertainty, control case and raw-data retention.
7. **Biupiu priority examples (7:30–9:30):** Hybrid VAWT, low-head hydro, farm energy, hemp processing and desiccant cooling; clearly label them as queued research, not completed results.
8. **Closing (9:30–10:30):** Invite viewers to follow documented iterations; explain that negative results are retained.

## Source and rights controls

Third-party video, photographs, diagrams, music, code and datasets require a recorded permission or compatible licence. Attribution alone is not treated as reuse permission. When a third-party clip is essential, prefer linking to the original source and explaining it with original visuals rather than republishing it.

## Technical validation checklist

- Claim has a unique Research ID.
- Evidence class is assigned.
- Primary/peer-reviewed/patent sources are recorded where available.
- Creator and assignee identity are recorded when identifiable.
- Patent and licence status are not assumed.
- Simulation and physical results are labelled separately.
- Units, boundary conditions and energy accounting are visible.
- Raw data, code version, configuration and date are retained.
- Corrections and limitations are documented.
