## Claim-testing rule

Sepehr videos are indexed as **source material for claims**, not as proof.

Each claim becomes:

SOURCE → CLAIM → HISTORICAL EVIDENCE → COUNTER-EVIDENCE → HYPOTHESIS → TESTABLE PREDICTION → EXPERIMENT/RESEARCH → RESULT

Claims involving extraordinary technologies, ancient global civilisations, lost continents, bloodlines, inner Earth, Vril/scalar energy or similar subjects remain explicitly labelled SPECULATIVE unless independently supported.
