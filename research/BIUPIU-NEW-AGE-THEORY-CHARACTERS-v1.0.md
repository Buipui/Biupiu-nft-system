# Biupiu World — New Age Theory Character & Avatar System v1.0

**Status:** Character/world-building specification  
**Purpose:** Give the New Age Theory gateway recognisable non-player characters, guides, researchers and player-avatar archetypes while keeping historical people, folklore and fictional representations clearly separated.

## Entry-point characters

### NAT-GUIDE-01 — The Archivist
Role: Main Hub / New Age Theory gateway guide.
Visual language: premium futuristic research attire, archive tablet, layered map/globe interface.
Function: explains evidence states, source provenance and navigation.
Classification: FICTIONAL BIOUPIU WORLD CHARACTER.

### NAT-GUIDE-02 — The Cartographer
Role: Ancient-map and lost-land research guide.
Visual language: explorer/researcher clothing, historical maps, GIS overlays and transparent terrain models.
Function: routes users into Tartaria, Thule, Hy-Brasil, Atlantis and submerged-land research.
Classification: FICTIONAL BIOUPIU WORLD CHARACTER.

### NAT-GUIDE-03 — The Geophysicist
Role: Hollow Earth / geology reality-check guide.
Visual language: modern field-science clothing, seismic/geophysical instrumentation and Earth cross-section interface.
Function: contrasts speculative inner-Earth models with documented geophysics.
Classification: FICTIONAL BIOUPIU WORLD CHARACTER.

### NAT-GUIDE-04 — The Archaeotechnology Researcher
Role: Ancient technology claim-testing guide.
Visual language: field archaeologist + engineer, measurement tools, material samples and reconstruction interface.
Function: sends claims to archaeology, materials, metallurgy, geometry and experimental laboratories.
Classification: FICTIONAL BIOUPIU WORLD CHARACTER.

## Character-generation constraints

- Characters must not be presented as actual historical inhabitants unless independently documented and explicitly identified.
- Legendary characters/entities may be represented as folklore-inspired visualisations, not documentary portraits.
- No invented quotation may be attributed to a historical or religious figure.
- Clothing, architecture, tools and environments are labelled as reconstructed, inspired or fictional where evidence is incomplete.
- Characters inherit the user's subscriber department permissions.
- A character cannot bypass evidence or department access controls.
- AI-generated character dialogue must cite the underlying source/research object when making factual historical claims.
- Speculative dialogue must be marked as hypothesis, tradition, folklore or fiction as appropriate.
