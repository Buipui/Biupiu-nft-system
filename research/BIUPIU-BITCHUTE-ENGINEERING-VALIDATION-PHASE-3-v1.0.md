# Biupiu BitChute Engineering Validation Programme — Phase 3

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 17 September 2026  
**Status:** EXECUTED — repository integration / prototype-queue advancement  
**Programme:** Biupiu BitChute engineering-validation stream

## 1. Phase 3 objective

Phase 3 converts the Phase 2 evidence register into a reproducible engineering-validation workflow. Each candidate design is now tracked through:

`SOURCE → CLAIM → EVIDENCE → PRIOR ART/IP → MODEL → BENCH PROTOTYPE → MEASUREMENT → REPEATABILITY → DESIGN ITERATION → RELEASE/DEFER`

BitChute material remains a discovery/input layer. It is not itself evidence of technical validity.

## 2. Priority prototype tracks

### P3-VAWT — Hybrid VAWT

**Research question:** Can a Biupiu-specific hybrid vertical-axis configuration improve self-starting behaviour, torque continuity or useful power in low/turbulent wind without copying protected prior art?

**Phase-3 gates:**
- establish geometry and baseline configuration;
- complete prior-art comparison before fabrication;
- build modular rotor and instrumentation;
- measure wind speed, RPM, torque, electrical output, start-up threshold, vibration and acoustic behaviour;
- calculate power coefficient with stated uncertainty;
- compare against a defined control rotor.

**Disposition:** PROTOTYPE QUEUE — controlled bench test.

### P3-HYDRO — Low-head micro-hydro

**Research question:** What power and efficiency are achievable over a measured low-head/flow envelope, including hydraulic losses and seasonal variation?

**Phase-3 gates:**
- measure gross and net head and flow;
- model runner/generator operating point;
- fabricate or acquire a non-infringing test runner;
- measure hydraulic power, electrical power and efficiency;
- test debris/sediment tolerance and safe shutdown;
- preserve site/resource assumptions separately from laboratory results.

**Disposition:** PROTOTYPE QUEUE — hydraulic test rig.

### P3-FARM-ENERGY — Integrated farm energy

**Research question:** Can measured agricultural loads be supplied reliably through coordinated renewable generation, storage and load control?

**Phase-3 gates:**
- collect real load profiles;
- model PV/wind/storage/load dispatch;
- implement a scaled controller/data logger;
- validate energy balance, state-of-charge behaviour, curtailment and peak-load response;
- compare model predictions with measured data.

**Disposition:** PROTOTYPE QUEUE — simulation + controller-in-the-loop.

### P3-HEMP — Hemp decortication/fractionation

**Research question:** Can a modular process produce repeatable bast-fibre/hurd separation at useful throughput while controlling dust, contamination and energy consumption?

**Phase-3 gates:**
- document feedstock moisture and stalk dimensions;
- establish mechanical process sequence;
- benchmark fibre/hurd yield and quality;
- measure throughput, energy, dust and noise;
- map each novel machine/process feature against patent families before commercialisation;
- preserve fibre quality and process data for downstream textile/composite validation.

**Disposition:** PROTOTYPE QUEUE — process/materials validation.

### P3-DESICCANT — Desiccant cooling

**Research question:** Can low-grade heat or solar thermal energy regenerate a modular desiccant system with useful moisture removal and acceptable parasitic energy consumption?

**Phase-3 gates:**
- select solid/liquid desiccant candidate;
- instrument temperature and humidity at inlet/outlet;
- measure moisture removal, regeneration temperature and energy input;
- calculate thermal/electrical COP using explicit boundaries;
- assess degradation, corrosion and carryover;
- calibrate a compact digital model against measurements.

**Disposition:** PROTOTYPE QUEUE — thermal test module.

## 3. Evidence and claim classification

- **VALIDATED ENGINEERING PRINCIPLE:** supported by established engineering literature and reproducible measurements.
- **SUPPORTED:** credible literature/patent/engineering evidence exists, but Biupiu has not independently reproduced the result.
- **EXPERIMENTAL:** Biupiu test required before performance is claimed.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** testable proposition with insufficient evidence.
- **SPECULATIVE:** creative/design inspiration only.
- **FALSIFICATION:** retained specifically to test or reject unsupported claims.

Free-energy/over-unity claims remain **FALSIFICATION ONLY**. They must not enter the performance baseline, funding assumptions or commercial product claims without a reproducible energy balance demonstrating an external energy source and accounting for measurement uncertainty.

## 4. CODEX/AAT/ALA/GEO/IP cross-links

**CODEX:** computational models, parameter files, test scripts, uncertainty calculations and reproducibility records.

**AAT:** ancient/vernacular engineering claims are recorded as historical technology questions, not proof of modern performance.

**ALA/GEO:** site/resource context, terrain, water flow, wind exposure, farm layout and geographic constraints.

**IP:** creator/assignee identity, patent family, priority date, jurisdiction, legal-status indicator and licence terms.

**Prior-art:** feature-by-feature comparison before fabrication and before any commercial claim of novelty.

**Experimental-validation:** hypothesis, independent/dependent variables, instrumentation, calibration, control case, uncertainty and repeatability.

## 5. Licensing / attribution rule

BitChute videos are treated as third-party source material unless the uploader is demonstrably the rights holder and grants a compatible licence. The programme does not assume that educational language, attribution or a platform upload creates a reuse licence.

For each source record, capture:
- original creator/uploader;
- original work title and date;
- source URL;
- ownership/assignee where identifiable;
- stated licence, if any;
- patent status where applicable;
- copyright/reuse status;
- Biupiu transformation/use decision.

Only original Biupiu recordings, diagrams, measurements and code are treated as Biupiu-created assets unless a separate licence record says otherwise.

## 6. Real-world prototype release gate

No design progresses from prototype to public performance claim until:

1. prior art has been checked;
2. safety hazards are recorded;
3. test instrumentation is defined;
4. calibration/uncertainty is recorded;
5. a control case exists where practical;
6. raw data are retained;
7. results are reproducible or limitations are explicitly documented;
8. IP/licensing status is recorded.
