## Selection criteria

A video can enter the curated Biupiu syllabus only after review for:
- factual quality
- date/context
- disclosure of sponsorship
- risk warnings
- platform accuracy
- absence of guaranteed-return claims
- absence of manipulative trading instructions
- educational value

## Biupiu video strategy

Biupiu's own financial/economic videos should use:
**crops + farms + barter + treasury + visual market simulations**

Example:
**"One Basket of Maize: How Supply, Scarcity and Markets Work"**

This preserves the Biupiu regenerative-agriculture identity while teaching economics.

## Regulatory resource

The FSCA provides consumer financial education and information intended to help South Africans make informed financial decisions. Its current website also states that crypto asset service providers require authorisation to conduct business.

Third-party content is reference material only; Biupiu should not imply endorsement of a creator, broker, exchange or trading strategy.
