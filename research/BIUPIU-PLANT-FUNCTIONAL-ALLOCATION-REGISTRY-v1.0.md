# Biupiu Plant Functional Allocation Registry v1.0

Date: 18 September 2026  
Purpose: allocate plant species/feedstocks to departments by experimentally testable functional properties.

## Allocation principle
A plant is assigned because a measurable property or process can support that department. Allocation is not a claim of product approval or therapeutic efficacy without testing.

## Department packages
AERO / EVTOL / HELICOPTER: hemp + flax primary; bamboo, kenaf, PALF and cork secondary. Use lightweight composite skins, fairings, ducts and secondary structures; flight-critical structures remain qualification-gated.
MARINE: hemp + flax with basalt hybrid; cork/coir for selected damping and impact applications.
ENERGY / MICRO-TURBINES: hemp + flax for qualified cold-structure blades; bio-based resin as matrix R&D; bamboo/kenaf/PALF secondary; basalt for critical-load hybrid reinforcement.
TEXTILES: hemp, flax, kenaf, jute, PALF, banana and cotton.
MATERIALS / COMPOSITES: hemp, flax, bamboo, sisal, kenaf, jute, PALF and coir; rice husk and bagasse as fillers; bio-based matrices.
BIOCARBON / BIOCHEM: hemp biomass, bagasse, rice husk, sunflower stalks and validated residues.
AGRI / LIVING SYSTEMS: hemp, vetiver, legumes, bamboo and residue systems.
WATER: vetiver and root-architecture plants plus experimentally justified biomass/filter materials.
BIOMED: evidence-based cellulose and plant-polymer biomaterial research only; this registry creates no therapeutic claim.
ROBOTICS / ADV-MFG / AI: all plant materials become manufacturing and inspection datasets.
