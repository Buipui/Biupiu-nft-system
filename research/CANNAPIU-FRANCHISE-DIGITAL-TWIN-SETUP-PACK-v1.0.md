**Date:** 20 September 2026

## Mission

CannaPiu is a new integrated Biupiu franchise division modelled on the Dankery Members Club platform. It combines a modular mobile hospitality platform with Biupiu-manufactured materials, automation, software, sensors, DMS and Biupiu Intelligence.

## Modular product system

1. **Vehicle shell and panels** — lightweight composite sandwich panels, replaceable service panels, standardised fasteners and inspection zones.
2. **Bio-resins and protective coatings** — resin matrix candidates and abrasion/UV/moisture/chemical exposure test coupons; no performance claim is accepted without measured test data.
3. **Bio-derived vinyls and PPF** — visual branding, surface protection, repairable wrap zones and substrate compatibility testing.
4. **Hemp canvas tent** — durable natural-fibre textile option with fire, weathering, seam, tensile, tear and mildew testing gates.
5. **Lightweight composite tent/awning** — modular poles, trusses, hinges, membrane attachments, ballast/anchoring and replaceable panels.
6. **Smart systems** — environmental sensors, load/tilt monitoring, access control, lighting, energy monitoring, equipment health and DMS telemetry.

## Digital-twin entities

- `SITE`: ground conditions, wind exposure, access and permitted footprint
- `VEHICLE`: chassis, mass, centre of gravity, panel interfaces and service loads
- `TENT`: geometry, fabric/composite elements, joints, anchors and ballast
- `AWNING`: span, cantilever, support geometry, wind and rain load cases
- `MATERIAL`: density, thickness, tensile/compressive/shear properties, safety factors and test evidence
- `SENSOR`: location, calibration, sampling rate, uncertainty and fault state
- `EVENT`: deployment, weather alert, inspection, fault, repair and decommissioning

## Commercial architecture

CannaPiu customers may purchase franchise rights, vehicle/trailer systems, tent/awning systems, packaging, branded materials, machinery, sensors, software, DMS subscriptions, maintenance and proprietary products. Internal transactions between Biupiu entities must be eliminated from consolidated revenue reporting.

## Safety boundary

The digital twin is a design and screening model, not certification. Final structures require a qualified structural engineer, applicable local code review, wind/fire/accessibility checks, anchoring verification, material testing and field commissioning.
