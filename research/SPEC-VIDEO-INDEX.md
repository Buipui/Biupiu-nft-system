## Purpose

This file indexes selected videos/pages from Vibes of Cosmos (VOC), Robert Sepehr / Atlantean Gardens, and Divergent Libertalia. These sources are **not treated as equivalent to archaeological, scientific or geodetic evidence**. They generate claims that can be converted into testable questions.

Research rule:

> Take the hypothesis seriously enough to test it, but not seriously enough to assume it is true.

## Evidence controls

- **A — Directly demonstrated:** independently verified physical/experimental evidence.
- **B — Strongly supported:** multiple reliable independent sources.
- **C — Plausible/incomplete:** credible mechanism or partial evidence.
- **D — Testable hypothesis:** claim can be operationalised and investigated.
- **E — Unsupported/contradicted:** presently lacks adequate evidence or conflicts with strong evidence.
- **M — Mythological/cultural:** useful as a historical/cultural source but not physical proof.

Creator ≠ source ≠ original evidence. Each record should trace the claim to its earliest identifiable source.
