# Biupiu Engineering Validation Video Series — Season 1

**Status:** Series framework created — production planning stage  
**Date:** 17 September 2026  
**Format:** Short technical explainers + laboratory/build logs + evidence reviews

## Editorial principles

1. Separate established engineering from hypotheses and speculation.
2. Cite source URLs, patents, papers and licences in episode notes.
3. Never present a simulation as a physical result.
4. Show energy input/output boundaries and measurement uncertainty.
5. Obtain permission for third-party footage, diagrams, music and excerpts.
6. Use original Biupiu narration, diagrams, CAD, test footage and data wherever possible.
7. Keep free-energy claims in a dedicated falsification episode/segment, not as demonstrated technology.

## Season 1 episode plan

### Episode 01 — From Video Claim to Engineering Test
- Introduce the BitChute source-index workflow.
- Explain evidence classes: documented, supported, experimental, hypothesis, speculative and falsification.
- Demonstrate claim decomposition and source genealogy.

### Episode 02 — Hybrid VAWT: What Can Actually Be Measured?
- Savonius/Darrieus operating principles.
- Self-starting, torque ripple, tip-speed ratio and power coefficient.
- Prior-art and safety gates.
- Bench-test instrumentation walkthrough.

### Episode 03 — Low-head Micro-hydro Without Overclaiming
- Head, flow, hydraulic power and losses.
- Site data versus laboratory data.
- Runner comparison and safe test-rig design.

### Episode 04 — Integrated Farm Energy System
- Agricultural load profiles.
- PV/wind/storage/load control.
- Data logging, dispatch and energy-balance verification.

### Episode 05 — Hemp Decortication and Fractionation
- Feedstock preparation and moisture.
- Bast fibre/hurd separation.
- Yield, fibre quality, dust, noise and energy metrics.
- Patent and licensing checkpoints.

### Episode 06 — Desiccant Cooling Using Low-grade Heat
- Humidity ratio and moisture removal.
- Solid versus liquid desiccants.
- Solar/waste-heat regeneration.
- COP boundaries and durability tests.

### Episode 07 — Patents, Prior Art and Creator Attribution
- How to identify original creators and assignees.
- Patent family, priority date and jurisdiction.
- Copyright, open licences and commercial-use constraints.
- Why attribution is not automatically a reuse licence.

### Episode 08 — Free-energy Claims: Falsification Lab
- Define the system boundary.
- Calibrate instruments and account for stored energy.
- Test repeatability and measurement uncertainty.
- Report negative or inconclusive results transparently.

### Episode 09 — From CAD to Prototype
- Design freeze and bill of materials.
- Risk assessment and test procedures.
- Control cases, data schemas and versioned CAD/code.

### Episode 10 — Phase 4 Review: Results, Limitations and Next Iteration
- Compare predictions with measurements.
- Record failures and design changes.
- Decide whether each concept advances, repeats, pauses or closes.
