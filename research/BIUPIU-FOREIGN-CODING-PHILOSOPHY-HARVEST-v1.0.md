## Imported philosophy
1. Choose the smallest useful workflow.
2. Lock scope and authority before execution.
3. Keep planning state durable and resumable.
4. Treat capability discovery as first-class.
5. Make dependencies and transitions explicit.
6. Parallelise only where dependencies permit.
7. Preserve execution history and evidence.
8. Verify on the artifact's real surface.
9. Keep human promotion gates explicit.
10. Separate orchestration from domain logic.
11. Prefer replaceable adapters over vendor coupling.
12. Keep loops bounded, observable and regression-tested.
