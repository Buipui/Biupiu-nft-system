## 9. Exterminate protocol

Duplicate-looking sources were **not destructively deleted**. They were classified:
- duplicate/overlapping research → consolidate as a single indexed source family;
- active proprietary patent → reference only;
- expired/lapsed patent → prior-art/FTO candidate, not assumed public-domain implementation;
- open-source GitHub project → external dependency candidate until licence/security/compatibility gates pass;
- research article → evidence/reference, not implementation;
- government/NASA/DARPA material → authoritative research reference, not Biupiu-owned IP.

No third-party code, assets, patents, datasets or confidential material are represented as Biupiu-owned by this harvest.
