## Purpose

Create an explicit bridge between the Biupiu 3D Computational Geometry (CG-3D), Photonic Holography & Quantitative-Phase Modelling (PH-QPM), and Photonics/Optical Communications (PHO) streams and Wits University optical-communications research.

## Wits-linked research themes

- Long-range free-space optical communication
- Visible-light communication / LiFi
- Structured-light communications
- Orbital-angular-momentum and spatial-mode encoding
- Topological/skyrmion light
- Atmospheric turbulence and propagation
- Robust optical eigenmodes
- Machine learning / AI-assisted optical communications
- Optical signal processing
- Optical and photonic computing
- Optical V2X and hybrid communication concepts

Wits research provides an experimental benchmark for studying structured optical fields in realistic atmospheric channels, including turbulence and mode propagation.

## 3D holography connection

`3D geometry → complex optical field → propagation → hologram / phase map → reconstructed volume → quantitative comparison`

Relevant variables include wavelength, phase, amplitude, propagation distance, refractive index, spatial mode, aperture, turbulence parameters, sampling/pixel pitch, reconstruction algorithm and signal-to-noise ratio.

## CG-3D connection

CG-3D supplies parameterised geometry for optical modelling:

- torus and toroidal surfaces
- helices and spirals
- polyhedra
- fractal/geometric forms
- architectural reconstructions
- topological surfaces
- nested geometries
- field-aligned geometries

The same geometry can be exported into optical, electromagnetic and visualisation simulations so artwork and engineering models retain a traceable geometric origin.

## Proposed experiments

### WITS-PHO-01 — Structured-light turbulence benchmark

Simulate selected spatial modes through controlled turbulence and compare mode fidelity against a conventional Gaussian-beam control.

### WITS-PHQPM-01 — Holographic reconstruction benchmark

Generate a known 3D parametric object, calculate its optical field/hologram, reconstruct it numerically and compare recovered geometry with the source mesh.

### WITS-CG-01 — Geometry-to-wavefront mapping

Map toroidal and other parametric surfaces into phase/amplitude distributions and investigate how geometry affects propagation and reconstruction quality.

### WITS-TORUS-01 — Toroidal structured-light geometry

Test whether a toroidal spatial structure can be represented and propagated as a structured optical field, without assuming an anomalous physical effect.

### WITS-AI-01 — AI turbulence compensation

Evaluate machine-learning prediction/correction of turbulence-induced phase or mode distortion against conventional signal-processing baselines.

## Evidence classification

- **Established / supported:** Structured optical fields, atmospheric turbulence and optical communications can be studied experimentally.
- **Plausible model:** Computational mapping between 3D geometry and optical phase/wavefront representations.
- **Research question:** Whether particular toroidal or topological geometries provide measurable advantages for a defined communication or reconstruction metric.
- **Speculative:** Unsupported claims assigning energy-generation or universal-field properties to geometric forms.
