# Biupiu Crystal Claim-to-Test Register v1.0

## Purpose
Create a structured bridge between collected speculative claims and measurable experiments.

## Record schema
- Claim ID
- Original source
- Source type
- Region/environment
- Claim wording
- Evidence status
- Proposed mechanism
- Testable prediction
- Apparatus
- Measurement
- Controls
- Reproduction status
- Result
- Conclusion
- Asset/experiment links

## Initial claim families
1. Ancient crystal optics
2. Crystal resonance
3. Piezoelectric effects
4. Crystal energy-storage concepts
5. Crystal-based photonic concepts
6. Geometric structures alleged to amplify or focus energy
7. Atlantean/Mu “Great Crystal” narratives

The final two remain speculative until supported by reproducible evidence.
