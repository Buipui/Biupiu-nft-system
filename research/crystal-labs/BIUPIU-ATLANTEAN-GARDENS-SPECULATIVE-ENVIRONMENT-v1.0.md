# Biupiu Atlantean Gardens — Speculative Environment v1.0

**Environment:** Atlantis/Mu-inspired creative research world
**Classification:** SPECULATIVE WORLD-BUILDING

## Concept
A garden/laboratory environment inspired by modern Atlantis narratives: water channels, terraces, reflective surfaces, crystal-display structures, mineral gardens, botanical research zones and geometric architecture.

It is a visualisation and experimental-design environment, not a reconstruction of a verified ancient city.

## Research zones
1. Water and hydraulic garden
2. Crystal observation garden
3. Mineral and geological garden
4. Light/optics garden
5. Botanical/plant research garden
6. Geometry and architecture zone
7. Energy-hypothesis workshop
8. Materials testing pavilion

## Research separation
Historical evidence, modern engineering, creative world-building and speculative claims are separate layers. Claims about real Atlantean or Mu technology enter the claim-to-test protocol before being treated as experiments.

## Biupiu World integration
The garden functions as a visual gateway between the MU speculative sandbox and modern Crystal/Materials Innovation Labs.
