## Evidence controls

- A — Directly demonstrated
- B — Strongly supported

- D — Testable hypothesis
- E — Unsupported/contradicted
- M — Mythological/cultural

## Important controls

1. Do not infer historical truth from repeated YouTube claims.
2. Do not count channels that reproduce the same source as independent corroboration.
3. Preserve the distinction between mythology, historical tradition, archaeological evidence and engineering hypothesis.
4. Do not use speculative claims as NFT facts; use them only as inspiration until independently validated.
5. Copyrighted videos, images, maps and text remain third-party material; Biupiu outputs should be original analytical work or properly licensed material.
6. Political, racial or ethnocentric interpretations are not accepted as evidence; test concrete historical propositions independently.
7. The absence of an archaeological record is not automatically proof of non-existence, but claims must remain appropriately graded when evidence is missing.
