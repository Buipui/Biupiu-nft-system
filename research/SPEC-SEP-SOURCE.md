# BIUPIU Robert Sepehr / Atlantean Gardens Source Registry

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 15 September 2026  
**Status:** Active — hypothesis-generation source layer

## Purpose

Maintain a dedicated source record for Robert Sepehr's Atlantean Gardens archive and associated videos. The archive is used to extract speculative claims for controlled investigation; it is not treated as a substitute for primary archaeology, peer-reviewed science, historical documentation, geology or geodesy.

## Source classification

Primary role: speculative anthropology, alternative ancient-history interpretation, mythology, human-origins hypotheses, ancient geography and lost-civilisation hypotheses.

Evidence rule: **creator claim ≠ primary evidence**. Every important claim must be traced to the earliest identifiable text, artefact, dataset or observation and independently tested.

## Research families

- `SEP-ATL` — Atlantis, antediluvian civilisation and catastrophe hypotheses.
- `SEP-HYP` — Hyperborea, Thule and northern ancient geography.
- `SEP-HUM` — human origins, archaic populations, migration and ancient DNA claims.
- `SEP-AMR` — Olmec, Mesoamerica, pre-Columbian contact and transoceanic hypotheses.
- `SEP-EGY` — Egypt, pyramids, temples and subterranean-architecture claims.
- `SEP-UND` — Agartha, Shambhala, inner-Earth and tunnel/cave traditions.
- `SEP-ANT` — Antarctica and historical/alternative geography claims.
- `SEP-TECH` — ancient technology, sacred geometry, energy and engineering claims.
- `SEP-VRIL` — Vril/scalar/free-energy claims; historical-belief status unless independently reproducible.
- `SEP-CAT` — catastrophism, resets and civilisational discontinuity.

## Cross-reference protocol

For each video/claim:

`video → timestamp → exact claim → original source → primary evidence → JSTOR → AnthroSource → ResearchGate → MDPI → Emerald → archaeology/geology/genetics → GIS/CODEX test → evidence status`

## Evidence controls

- A — Directly demonstrated
- B — Strongly supported
- C — Plausible/incomplete
- D — Testable hypothesis
- E — Unsupported/contradicted
- M — Mythological/cultural

## Important controls

1. Do not infer historical truth from repeated YouTube claims.
2. Do not count channels that reproduce the same source as independent corroboration.
3. Preserve the distinction between mythology, historical tradition, archaeological evidence and engineering hypothesis.
4. Do not use speculative claims as NFT facts; use them only as inspiration until independently validated.
5. Copyrighted videos, images, maps and text remain third-party material; Biupiu outputs should be original analytical work or properly licensed material.
