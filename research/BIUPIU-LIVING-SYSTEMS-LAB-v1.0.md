# Biupiu Living Systems Laboratory v1.0

## Scope
Controlled research architecture for regenerative and biological systems.

## Tracks
- regenerative agriculture
- soil biology
- plant interactions
- water systems
- biochar
- fungi
- carbon cycling
- hemp
- agroforestry
- ecological restoration
- natural materials

Experiments should record controls, environmental conditions, biological variability, safety requirements and applicable regulatory/ethical review.
