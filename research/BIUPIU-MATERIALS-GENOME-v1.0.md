# Biupiu Materials Genome v1.0

A structured registry for material composition, structure, properties, processing, cost, testing, safety, prior art and applications.

## Record schema
`MATERIAL-ID | FAMILY | SOURCE | COMPOSITION | STRUCTURE | PROPERTIES | PROCESS | EQUIPMENT | COST | TESTS | SAFETY | IP/PRIOR ART | APPLICATIONS | COMPATIBILITY | EVIDENCE STATE`

## Initial families
Hemp fibre; biochar/carbon; natural-fibre composites; biodegradable adhesives; coatings; ceramics; advanced carbon materials; photonic/crystal materials; metamaterials/metasurfaces; regenerative-agriculture materials.

All properties must retain source provenance and test conditions.
