# Biupiu CROSS-DEPARTMENT MATRIX v1.0

**Status:** Architecture specification
**Date:** 18 September 2026

## Purpose

Navigation architecture: MAIN HUB → DEPARTMENT SPACE → CULTURAL ENVIRONMENT → SPECIALIST WORKSHOP/LAB → PROJECT → RESEARCH OBJECT. METALLURGY, TEXTILES, MATERIALS and FARMING each retain their own department boundaries. Cultural heritage is an organisational/educational layer, not proof of a historical or scientific claim. Evidence states remain DOCUMENTED, RECONSTRUCTED, EXPERIMENTAL, HYPOTHESIS or SPECULATIVE. Search, AI retrieval, projects and exports inherit the selected department boundary.

## Environment rule

Each environment combines documented cultural context with a specialist workspace. Modern experiments are recorded separately from historical reconstruction. Cross-department resources require explicit SHARED classification.
