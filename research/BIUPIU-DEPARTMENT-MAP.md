## Purpose

This map connects the master department registry to the specialist research streams, known repository records, prior Wits/ResearchGate research, Emerald Insight cross-reference work, IP/prior-art and public/declassified-source work, and the Biupiu commercial/R&D boundary.

**Important:** A department assignment is an indexing relationship, not proof that a research claim is validated. Existing specialist IDs remain authoritative for their source records. Where a prior register or upload is referenced from earlier project work but a standalone file is not currently discoverable, the status is explicitly marked **VERIFY SOURCE FILE**.

## Cross-reference rules for external research

### ResearchGate
Use ResearchGate as a literature-discovery and cross-reference layer. It is not itself treated as the primary authority where the original publisher, repository or institutional record is available. The current Wits structured-light records include the 2023 *Robust structured light in atmospheric turbulence* paper and the 2025 digital-implementation tutorial. citehttps://www.researchgate.net/publication/368349544_Robust_structured_light_in_atmospheric_turbulencehttps://www.researchgate.net/publication/388762227_Structured_light_in_atmospheric_turbulence-a_guide_to_its_digital_implementation_tutorial

### Emerald Insight
Emerald Insight is a complementary literature-discovery source. Future records should capture the exact Emerald title/DOI, publication year, author/institution and research relevance, then cross-check against the primary publisher or original dataset before assigning an evidence status. A generic Emerald source mention must not be treated as independent corroboration until the underlying publication is identified.

### Wits University / previous study uploads
The Wits chain is now an explicit PHOTONICS/PH-QPM/CODEX reference layer. Current public Wits material confirms work on structured light, turbulence mitigation, long-range free-space optical links, programmable optics and a 300 m campus optical testbed; the 25 August 2026 Wits report also documents a real-world experiment using topological information in light under atmospheric turbulence. citehttps://www.wits.ac.za/oclab/research/https://www.wits.ac.za/news/latest-news/research-news/2026/--2026-08/wits-researchers-use-lights-topology-to-beat-atmospheric-distortion.html

The previously uploaded Wits study material is therefore mapped to **PHOTONICS → PH-QPM → COMPUTE/CODEX → AI → DIGITAL-TWIN**, with secondary links to ELECTROMAG and GEOMETRY. Wits source records remain primary institutional references where available; ResearchGate is the discovery/cross-reference layer.

## Declassified patent / public technical-document treatment

The IP layer includes public/declassified engineering and patent-document research. These records must remain separated from classified or non-public military information. Patent documents are prior-art evidence, not proof of performance; declassified documents are historical/public technical sources and require engineering validation before reuse.

Mapping priority:

`IP-PA / DECLASS → AERO / MARINE / MATERIALS / ENERGY / ELECTROMAG / GEOMETRY / COMPUTE`

For aerospace and propulsion subjects, only public, officially released/declassified, licensed or independently generated information may be indexed.

## Activation status key

- **ACTIVE** — part of an active Biupiu research or implementation stream.
- **R&D** — research/prototype stage.
- **PERSONAL R&D** — retained in the separate personal technical/IP portfolio.
- **COMMERCIAL CORE** — directly relevant to the current commercial deployment subset.
- **SPEC** — controlled speculative source layer.
- **VERIFY SOURCE FILE** — relationship known from prior project work but source file not presently identified.

## Governance

This mapping does not merge the commercial DTIC/InvestSA library with the personal R&D/NFT repository. It creates cross-links while preserving separate disclosure, evidence, licensing and funding gates.
