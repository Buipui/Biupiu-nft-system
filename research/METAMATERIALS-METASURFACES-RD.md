## Purpose

It covers electromagnetic, optical, acoustic, mechanical and multifunctional metamaterial systems, including metasurfaces and architected materials.

## Simulation Branches

### MM-01 — Electromagnetic Unit Cell

Model metamaterial unit cells using FEM/FDTD or equivalent validated numerical methods. Compare engineered geometry against a conventional-material control.

Outputs: S-parameters, effective-medium estimates where justified, field distribution, resonance frequency, Q-factor, loss and bandwidth.

### MM-02 — Metasurface Wavefront Control

Model phase/amplitude/polarization response of a metasurface array.

Outputs: phase profile, far-field pattern, focusing efficiency, polarization conversion and bandwidth.

### MM-03 — Reconfigurable Metamaterials

Sweep tunable parameters such as geometry, material state, applied electric field, temperature or other physically justified control variables.

Outputs: tuning range, hysteresis if present, insertion loss, response time and repeatability.

### MM-04 — Mechanical Architected Materials

Compare lattice/architected geometries against conventional material controls.

Outputs: mass density, stiffness, strength, energy absorption, buckling modes, vibration response and failure modes.

### MM-05 — Metamaterial Antennas / Beam Steering

Investigate engineered waveguides, lenses, resonant surfaces and beamforming geometries.

Outputs: radiation pattern, gain, efficiency, bandwidth, steering angle and sidelobe behaviour.

### MM-06 — Metamaterial PV / Optical Energy

Evaluate whether engineered surfaces improve absorption, angular response, spectral selectivity or optical coupling relative to conventional controls.

Outputs: optical absorption, spectral response, irradiance uniformity, thermal loading and electrical conversion metrics.

### MM-07 — Toroidal Metamaterials

Use CG-3D and TORUS parameterisation to investigate toroidal unit cells, resonant geometries and metasurface patterns.

Outputs: multipole content, field concentration, resonant response, coupling and comparison against non-toroidal controls.

### MM-08 — Inverse Design / Topology Optimisation

Use numerical optimisation to search for structures meeting a specified electromagnetic, optical or mechanical target.

Outputs: objective function, constraints, Pareto trade-offs, manufacturability and robustness to dimensional/material tolerances.

## Evidence Discipline

A simulation is not proof of a physical effect. A NASA project, patent, funded proposal or declassified document is not automatically experimental validation. Claims must be classified using the repository evidence framework:

- Established
- Supported Research
- Plausible Model
- Unresolved
- Inconsistent
- Speculative / Inspirational
