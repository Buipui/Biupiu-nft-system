# Biupiu Public Declassified Resource Registry

## Scope
Publicly released/declassified research only. This registry is a research/evidence layer,
not an operational intelligence integration.

## Authoritative discovery sources
- CIA FOIA Electronic Reading Room
- NSA FOIA / Declassification & Transparency Initiatives
- National Archives CIA records
- FOIA.gov developer/search resources
- USPTO Patent Public Search / Open Data Portal

## Candidate module families
- historical cryptography and communications
- document/image OCR and metadata extraction
- geospatial and remote-sensing analysis
- signal-processing and information-theory research
- historical systems engineering and reliability
- patent prior-art / technology-landscape analysis
- provenance, citation and evidence-chain indexing

## Promotion gate
Every candidate must pass: public-source verification, provenance, licence/rights review,
security review, dependency review, regression tests, reproducibility checks, and human
promotion approval. Classified, leaked, unlawfully obtained, credentialed, or restricted
material is excluded.
