# Biupiu Digital Factory v1.0

## Scope
Digital planning and simulation architecture for future advanced manufacturing.

## Modules
- CNC and digital fabrication
- additive manufacturing / 3D printing
- composites
- hemp and textile processing
- robotics
- machine vision
- automated inspection
- sensors and instrumentation
- BOM and production planning
- manufacturing constraints
- digital twins
- process optimisation

Physical capability must not be implied until equipment, facilities and operating procedures actually exist.
