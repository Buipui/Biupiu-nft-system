# Biupiu External Collaboration Layer v1.0

## Collaboration classes
- universities and research groups
- laboratories
- engineers and technical specialists
- manufacturers
- technology providers
- standards bodies
- investors
- government programmes

## Record fields
`PARTNER | ORGANISATION | CAPABILITY | PUBLIC/PRIVATE | SOURCE | DATE | PROJECT LINKS | DATA/KNOWLEDGE SHARED | IP/CONFIDENTIALITY | NEXT ACTION`

Public information must remain separate from confidential/private information. External claims require source and date.
