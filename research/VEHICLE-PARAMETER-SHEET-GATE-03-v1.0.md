### Hypercar candidate powertrain inputs

- YASA's 750R data sheet reports 200 kW peak at 700 V, up to 70 kW continuous power at 3,000 rpm under its stated cooling conditions, 790 Nm peak torque at 450 A RMS, 37 kg mass and 368 mm diameter × 98 mm axial length. citeturn4search12
- YASA's current product page describes the 750R as 200 kW peak and 700 Nm peak torque, 0–3,250 rpm and 98 mm axial length; the difference from the older datasheet's 790 Nm figure is retained rather than reconciled by assumption. citeturn4search0turn4search12
- Therefore two 750R motors have a nominal combined peak rating of 400 kW and a catalogued motor mass of 74 kg before inverters, cabling, cooling, mounts and other integration hardware. This is a calculated combination of published motor figures, not a vehicle-level performance claim. citeturn4search12

### Audi 4.2 TDI

- Audi's published records confirm 4.2 TDI applications and 283 kW output for the A8 4.2 TDI in later model years. citeturn1search7turn1search9
- A supplier/OEM-derived parts catalogue identifies the Q7 4.2 TDI CCFA/CCFC at 250 kW and 4134 cc. citeturn2search28
- The exact CCFA versus CKDA engine-code configuration, mass, complete dimensions, accessory arrangement and suitability for the proposed hypercar package remain OPEN. The repository must not treat CKDA as the preferred engine until primary engine-specific documentation is obtained.

## Conservative mass controls

No complete vehicle mass is promoted. Required measured/sourced line items remain:

1. donor chassis/body/structure;
2. engine and all ancillaries;
3. transmission/differentials/shafts;
4. motors, inverters, battery and HV protection;
5. cooling and thermal systems;
6. composite panels, joints, glazing and closures;
7. brakes, steering, wheels and tyres;
8. seats, restraints and safety structures;
9. wiring, controls, sensors, fluids and service margins.
