## Research Objective

Establish a documented research base for Biupiu computational-art systems, with emphasis on geometry, symmetry, repetition, proportion, generative construction and the responsible use of historical/ancient engineering references.

## Research Questions

1. Which mathematical systems produce visually coherent radial and recursive patterns?
2. How can parameterized geometry be converted into reproducible generative artwork?
3. Which historical pattern and engineering examples can be supported by reliable sources?
4. How should historical references be separated from modern artistic interpretation?
5. How can source parameters and hashes provide reproducibility and provenance?

## Research Record Structure

For each source, record:

- Author / researcher
- Institution
- Publication / title
- Publication date
- URL or DOI where available
- Relevant page/section
- Research finding
- Relevance to Biupiu
- Related artwork/IP ID
- Notes on confidence or limitations

## Initial Research Tracks

- Computational geometry
- Generative / algorithmic art
- Mathematical symmetry and tessellation
- Radial and recursive systems
- Ancient architectural pattern research
- Historical engineering systems
- Digital provenance and reproducible generation

## Evidence Policy

Historical or technical claims must be supported by identifiable sources before they are incorporated into public artwork descriptions. Artistic interpretation must be clearly distinguished from documented historical fact.
