# BIUPIU — Vibes of Cosmos YouTube Index

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 15 September 2026  
**Status:** Permanent speculative-source index

## Purpose

This index records Vibes of Cosmos YouTube material as a hypothesis-generation source for the Biupiu Ancient World Atlas and speculative research system.

The channel is not treated as evidence equivalent to peer-reviewed science, archaeology, geodesy or official mapping. Each video is to be converted into claim-specific records and tested independently.

## Channel research categories

### VOC-YT-01 — Alternative Cartography
World maps, map projections, Pangaia, hidden lands, alternative coastlines and geographic models.

### VOC-YT-02 — Plasma Moon / Selenographic Mapping
Claims involving lunar imagery, lunar phases, visual geometry and reconstruction of terrestrial geography from lunar/cartographic observations.

### VOC-YT-03 — Antarctica / Terra Vista
Claims concerning Antarctica, ice walls, lands beyond Antarctica, hidden continents and alternative southern geography.

### VOC-YT-04 — Oceanografia
Seabed, underwater craters, ocean depth and submerged-land claims; test against authoritative bathymetry and geology.

### VOC-YT-05 — Cosmic Epochs
Cycle, chronology and cosmic-epoch hypotheses; convert claims into mathematically defined predictions before testing.

### VOC-YT-06 — Magnetic North / Great Circles
Magnetic-pole, great-circle and celestial-geography claims; compare with geomagnetic models, palaeomagnetism and spherical geometry.

### VOC-YT-07 — Ancient/Lost Lands
Pangaia, Atlantis, Hyperborea, Mu, Lemuria and related geographical traditions. Separate historical/mythological traditions from physical-land claims.

### VOC-YT-08 — Map Version History
Preserve each map/video version independently so geographic claims can be compared across releases.

## Evidence discipline

**Creator ≠ source ≠ original evidence.** Similar claims across VOC, Sepehr/Atlantean Gardens and Divergent are not counted as independent corroboration until source genealogy is established.

All physical-world claims must be converted into measurable predictions. Mythological or fictional geography is retained as cultural/source material and is not silently promoted to physical geography.
