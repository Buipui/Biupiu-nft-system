### Gate objective
Convert the newly incorporated anthropology/ancient-technology evidence layer into executable, provenance-controlled research queues without collapsing archaeological interpretation into engineering fact.

### Research protocol extension
For relevant questions, the Biupiu research stack now explicitly routes:

`PRIMARY ARCHAEOLOGY/HISTORY → JSTOR → ANTHROSOURCE → RESEARCHGATE/SCIENTIFIC DISCOVERY → PUBLISHER/DOI VERIFICATION → EMERALD IMPLEMENTATION → PATENTS/PRIOR ART → PUBLIC/DECLASSIFIED RECORDS → GITHUB/CODEX → COMPUTATIONAL TEST → EXPERIMENT → EVIDENCE STATUS → IP/NFT`

ResearchGate is treated as a discovery/repository layer. When a ResearchGate record points to a publisher, DOI, institutional repository or primary dataset, that authoritative source is preferred for the final evidence record. Duplicate uploads are one source lineage.

### New executable research records

**Blombos computational archaeology:** image/vector geometry, topology, null/alternative hypothesis testing and experimental reconstruction are now represented as a dedicated CODEX gate. Functional interpretations remain hypotheses until independent corroboration.

**Bokoni ancient-agriculture archaeology:** LiDAR/DEM/aerial imagery, georeferencing, terrace/field segmentation, environmental variables and archaeological controls are now routed into a Southern African landscape-reconstruction gate. Historical Bokoni evidence is kept separate from any modern regenerative-agriculture engineering claim.

**GeoAI archaeological discovery:** remote sensing + labelled archaeological data + spatially held-out evaluation + human archaeological verification is now a dedicated methodological gate.

**Retrospective archaeological photogrammetry:** archival photographs → 3D reconstruction → georeferencing → temporal layers is now linked to the Digital Ancient Technology Atlas and Digital Twin architecture.

### Tor / ResearchGate operating preference
The requested future workflow is recorded conceptually as **ResearchGate-first for scientific/engineering discovery, with primary/publisher verification and lawful public-source research**. ChatGPT cannot launch or operate a separate Tor Browser session, so Tor cannot be treated as a tool the repository workflow silently executes. Where public web research is appropriate, privacy-oriented browsing may be used by the user separately; source authority is determined by provenance and evidence quality, not by whether a source was accessed through Tor.
