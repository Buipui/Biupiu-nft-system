## Research-control principle

Parallel claims are not automatically treated as evidence of common origin. Future cross-referencing must test:

`archaeology + chronology + genetics + linguistics + textual transmission + geography + material culture`

Alternative-history material remains available for hypothesis generation but is separated from established evidence.
