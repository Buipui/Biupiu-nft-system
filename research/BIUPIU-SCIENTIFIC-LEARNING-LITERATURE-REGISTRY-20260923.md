## NASA — software assurance and architecture

- NASA Software Engineering Requirements / Standards / Handbook: lifecycle software engineering, assurance, safety, IV&V and secure coding.
- NASA Software Assurance and Software Safety Standard NASA-STD-8739.8B: systematic assurance and IV&V.
- NASA Software Engineering Handbook coding guidance: coding standards must be selected, adhered to and verified.
- NASA IV&V guidance: verification asks whether the product is built right; validation asks whether the right product is built.
- NASA F´: component-driven, multi-platform flight software with defined interfaces and unit/integration testing.

Biupiu learning use:
ARCHITECTURE -> REQUIREMENT TRACEABILITY -> COMPONENT CONTRACTS -> TESTABILITY -> ASSURANCE -> FAILURE LEARNING.

## DARPA — quantum / photonics / heterogeneous systems

- DARPA Quantum Sensing and Computing: quantum phenomena as both constraints and opportunities in computing/sensing.
- DARPA HARQ: heterogeneous quantum architectures combining different qubit types and quantum interconnects.
- DARPA LUMOS: heterogeneous integration of materials for scalable photonics, lasers and optical computing.
- DARPA PhENOM: quantum-correlated/loss-resilient states of light and nanoscale optical metrology.
- DARPA INSPIRED: squeezed-light quantum photonics for low-noise detection.
- DARPA ATOM: computational/material-discovery methods for tunable optical materials.
- DARPA 2026 It’s About Time: manufacturing/scaling of quantum and quantum-enabling optical-clock technology.
- DARPA Perrseus: manufacturable miniature vacuum systems combining MEMS, photonics and quantum sensing.

Biupiu learning use:
HETEROGENEOUS COMPUTE -> PHOTONICS -> MATERIALS -> QUANTUM STATE MODELS -> MANUFACTURING CONSTRAINTS -> SIMULATION.

## NIST — quantum, photonics and materials

- NIST Quantum Information Science: quantum physics + information theory for computing, sensing, communications and materials science.
- NIST Quantum Science: quantum sensors, quantum networking, single-photon detection, cryogenics and quantum-enabling technologies.
- NIST Advanced Microwave Photonics: superconducting quantum circuits, parametric coupling, optomechanics and hybrid quantum simulation.
- NIST Photonics and Optomechanics: integrated nano-optics, quantum photonics, meta-optics and photon/phonon interactions.
- NIST 2026 integrated photonics work: heterogeneous materials on silicon for compact photonic circuits and laser functionality.

Biupiu learning use:
PHOTONICS/MATERIALS MODELS -> OPTICAL PROPAGATION -> RESONATORS -> QUANTUM/CLASSICAL BOUNDARY -> METROLOGY -> DEVICE MODELS.

- U.S. DOE Storage Innovations 2030 supercapacitor assessment identifies biochar-derived activated carbon as a major materials pathway and lists hemp stalks among potential agricultural feedstocks.

Biupiu learning use:
BIOMASS -> PYROLYSIS/CARBON MODEL -> POROSITY/SURFACE/CONDUCTIVITY MODEL -> ELECTROCHEMICAL MODEL -> SUPERCAPACITOR SIMULATION -> EXPERIMENTAL VALIDATION.

Hemp-specific material claims remain RESEARCH/SUPPORTED or lower until the relevant experiment and performance measurements are captured.

## Evidence policy

Literature records are:
REFERENCE until source classification;
LEARNING INPUT when extracted into a governed knowledge record;
SIMULATION EVIDENCE when used in a model;
EXPERIMENTAL EVIDENCE only when actual measurements exist;
VERIFIED CAPABILITY only after the applicable execution/measurement gate.

No literature source, agency, paper, codebase or model becomes first-party Biupiu code merely because it is high quality or authoritative externally.
