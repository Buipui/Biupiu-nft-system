# NFT Research Stream — Ancient Systems × Computational Geometry

**Index ID:** NFT-ATH-GEO
**Status:** Active research/artwork-development stream
**Purpose:** Connect Biupiu ancient-technology and archaeological research with computational geometry and generative artwork for the founding NFT crowdfunding project.

## Scope

This stream links documented ancient technologies, archaeological evidence, historical engineering and cultural material with computational reconstruction, mathematical modelling, generative geometry and original digital artwork.

## Research-to-Art Model

Source → evidence classification → historical/technical interpretation → model → computational geometry → algorithm → generated artwork → provenance → NFT.

## Primary Themes

- Ancient water engineering and hydraulic systems
- Ancient irrigation and agricultural terraces
- Waru-waru and regenerative agricultural concepts
- Ancient mining and metallurgy
- Ceramics and material technologies
- Stone construction and architectural geometry
- Archaeoastronomy and orientation
- Ancient transportation and trade networks
- Southern African archaeological technologies
- Biological and natural geometry inspired by historical observations

## Computational Geometry Toolkit

Potential systems include circles, spirals, fractals, Voronoi structures, tessellation, symmetry, topology, polyhedra, flow fields, networks, phyllotaxis, recursion, parametric surfaces and meshes, and geometry-to-wavefront representations.

## Evidence Controls

Ancient technology claims are classified as DOCUMENTED, RECONSTRUCTED, EXPERIMENTAL, HYPOTHESIS or SPECULATIVE. Artistic interpretation must not be presented as archaeological or scientific proof.

## Cross-Research Links

Primary linkage targets include `ATH`, `ATH-H`, `ATH-HO-AFRICA`, `ATH-IN`, `ATH-SL`, `GEOARCH`, `LAND-GIS`, `ALA`, `PALAEO`, `COAST`, `GEO-MAG`, `CG-3D`, `CODEX`, `PH-QPM`, `PHO`, `MATERIALS`, `WATER`, `NFT-IP` and relevant invention/prior-art records.

## NFT Architecture

Each artwork receives a unique NFT ID and one or more Biupiu Research IDs. Computational pieces should preserve algorithm name/version, parameters, generation seed where applicable, software/dependencies, source-code status and asset/metadata hashes.

## IP Rule

Public source material is not automatically free of rights. Third-party images, code, datasets and research outputs must be licence-checked. Potentially patentable Biupiu inventions and confidential material must remain separated from public NFT artwork repositories until IP review is complete.

## Planned Founding Artwork Series

1. **Water** — ancient hydraulic systems transformed into computational flow geometry.
2. **Earth** — agricultural terraces, soil and landscape geometry.
3. **Stone** — architectural and megalithic geometry.
4. **Metal** — metallurgy, minerals and transformation.
5. **Sky** — astronomical orientation and geometric patterns.
6. **Network** — ancient settlements, trade and transport networks.
7. **Life** — biological geometry and traditional ecological systems.
8. **Transformation** — ancient systems reinterpreted as futuristic computational structures.

## Validation Principle

Ancient knowledge generates questions; archaeology establishes evidence; science identifies mechanisms; engineering makes them measurable; computation enables modelling and optimisation; Biupiu converts validated or clearly labelled exploratory work into original artwork.
