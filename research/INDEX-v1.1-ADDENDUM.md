## Research rule

Speculative sources generate hypotheses; archaeology, geology, geodesy, palaeoclimate, genetics, historical records, remote sensing and reproducible computation determine evidence status.

**Creator ≠ source ≠ original evidence.** Repeated claims are not independent corroboration until source genealogy demonstrates independence.

Protected third-party maps, videos, photographs and artwork are not copied into Biupiu NFT assets without rights. Biupiu-generated analytical outputs must retain provenance and licensing records.
