## Immediate CODEX tests

1. Implement a conventional Gaussian baseline.
2. Implement at least one structured-light mode.
3. Apply a defined atmospheric-turbulence model.
4. Compare received power, mode fidelity and/or BER under identical boundary conditions.
5. Record random seeds, wavelength, aperture, propagation distance, turbulence parameters and receiver assumptions.
6. Compare simulation outputs with published Wits results before claiming replication.
7. Only after validation, evaluate AI optimisation or digital-twin extensions.

## Evidence controls

- Do not interpret robustness of one optical encoding scheme as proof that all structured light is turbulence-immune.
- Distinguish numerical, laboratory and real-world field evidence.
- Preserve the original source DOI and institutional record.
- Respect the source licence before redistributing papers, figures or datasets.
- ResearchGate and Emerald discovery results are not automatically independent confirmations.

## IP / commercial gate

Any Biupiu implementation must undergo prior-art review before publication or patent filing. Wits-originated intellectual property remains the property of its owners unless separately licensed or independently developed. Biupiu may use public research as a research input while maintaining clear provenance and avoiding misrepresentation of institutional affiliation.
