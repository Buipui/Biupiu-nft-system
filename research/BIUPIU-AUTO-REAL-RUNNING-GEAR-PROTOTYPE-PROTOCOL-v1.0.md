# Biupiu Automotive Real-Running-Gear Prototype Protocol v1.0

**Date:** 20 September 2026  
**Priority:** HIGH  
**Status:** Gate executed at protocol level; donor selection and physical tests remain open

## Design decision

For early proof-of-concept vehicles, retain OEM-qualified running gear wherever practical: chassis/safety cell, suspension, steering, hubs, brakes, differential, half-shafts, transfer case and mechanical transmission. Biupiu materials are first evaluated in replaceable, non-crash-critical body, interior, acoustic, textile and service-panel products. This isolates materials innovation from safety-critical drivetrain uncertainty.

## Architecture suitability criteria

A candidate donor must be assessed for:

- Existing crash-tested safety cell and documented structural load paths
- Parts availability and workshop support
- Space for instrumentation and removable body-panel tooling
- Accessible suspension, brake and drivetrain service points
- CAN/diagnostic access where legally and technically appropriate
- Mass, wheelbase, track, axle loads and cooling envelope
- Registration, insurance, emissions and modification requirements
- Availability of duplicate panels for A/B comparison

## Prototype architecture families

### Hypercar / performance demonstrator

Use a longitudinal performance donor with intact OEM running gear and safety systems. Initially test Biupiu panels, trim, acoustic treatments and bonded joints while leaving steering, braking, suspension and shafts conventional. A later electrified front axle can be evaluated on a dedicated rig before vehicle installation.

### Expedition demonstrator

Use a ladder-frame 4x4 donor with OEM transfer case, axles, suspension, steering and brakes retained. Start with removable body panels, interior modules, protective covers and acoustic/thermal products. Evaluate the P2 motor-generator on a bench or rolling rig before integration into the live vehicle.

### Materials mule

Use a common, well-supported vehicle with low-cost replacement panels and straightforward access. This vehicle is for weathering, vibration, attachment, thermal and serviceability trials rather than high-speed structural claims.

## Running-gear substitution rule

No Biupiu composite replacement is permitted for steering, braking, suspension load paths, wheel hubs, half-shafts, crash structures or engine/transmission mounts during the first demonstrator phase without formal design review, destructive testing, failure containment and legal certification.

## Product test progression

1. Coupon: tensile, flexural, interlaminar, impact, moisture and thermal cycling.
2. Subcomponent: flat panels, bonded joints, fasteners, inserts and sandwich edges.
3. Vehicle panel: fit, vibration, water ingress, stone/debris exposure, UV and service removal.
4. Controlled vehicle test: low-speed handling, noise/vibration and thermal exposure only after inspection.
5. Engineering promotion: independent review, repeatability, traceability and regulatory assessment.

## Current evidence inputs

- ResearchGate: 2025 explicit dynamic analysis of hemp/flax car body panels; reports limitations involving strength, bonding and moisture, and tests tensile, flexural, impact, hardness and SEM responses.
- ResearchGate / Journal of Materials Science: textile-based natural-fibre composite automotive lightweighting review, DOI 10.1007/s10853-021-06509-6.
- Heliyon 2024 review: DOI 10.1016/j.heliyon.2024.e24683.
- Materials 2024 crashworthiness review: DOI 10.3390/ma17102246.
- Italian university thesis record: hybrid natural-fibre composites for racing bodywork, https://hdl.handle.net/20.500.12075/7490.
- ENEA Italian research record: PLA/flax/hemp commingled interior panels, https://hdl.handle.net/20.500.12079/6290.
- Fraunhofer WKI German research: natural-fibre bio-lightweight bodywork and hybrid bio-composites.
- German FNR project HIPSTER: hemp bast fibre with largely bio-based epoxidized vegetable-oil epoxy for an automotive demonstrator.

## Protocol status

The sources support a research and demonstrator route, not automatic approval for safety-critical structural use. Material properties must be measured for the exact fibre, matrix, processing route, conditioning state and geometry used by Biupiu.
