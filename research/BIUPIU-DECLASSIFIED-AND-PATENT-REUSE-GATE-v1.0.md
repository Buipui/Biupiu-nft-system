# Biupiu R&D OS — Declassified / Patent Reuse Gate v1.0

## Rule

Historical or declassified information may inform architecture and research, but declassification is not equivalent to a copyright, patent or software licence grant.

### Declassified sources

Track official release metadata, document identifier, release date, original classification, provenance and technical relevance.

Examples identified:
- CIA PC Software Justification (1987; released 2011)
- CIA Security of Agency PC Diskettes (1986; released 2012)
- CIA Agency Information Technology Standards (1986; released 2011)
- CIA Computer Security Initiative keynote (1981; released 2008)

These records can inform historical security, procurement, compatibility and governance research. They are not to be treated as reusable source code.

### Patent sources

Track jurisdiction, patent/application number, priority date, filing/grant dates, family, claims, legal status, estimated expiration and licence/assignment information.

A patent marked expired/lapsed in one jurisdiction does not automatically mean the entire family is free to use globally.

## AI rule

The AI may surface expired-patent candidates for human legal review. It must not state that a technology is legally free to commercialise until jurisdiction-specific legal status and other IP rights have been checked.

## Integration status

Every transition requires evidence and an audit record.
