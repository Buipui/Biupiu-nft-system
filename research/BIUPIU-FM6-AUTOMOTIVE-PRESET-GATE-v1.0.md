# Biupiu FM6 Automotive Preset Gate v1.0

**Gate:** FM6-02  
**Status:** EXECUTED — schema/reference layer

## Purpose
Convert FM6 graphics research into a machine-readable, rights-clean preset layer for original Biupiu automotive, aerospace and Digital Twin visualisation.

## Controls
- LOD policy is engine/provider configurable.
- Material values are presentation presets, not measured engineering properties.
- Normal/tangent validation is explicit.
- Camera framing and lens are creative/render controls.
- Lighting presets are presentation controls.
- Assets remain governed by the Universal Asset Interchange and visual-asset provenance rules.
- The reference-state field prevents this layer from becoming a Forza asset dependency.

## Digital Twin linkage
The preset is intended to attach to an existing visual asset manifest and Digital Twin/project ID. It does not alter authoritative engineering geometry or material allowables.

## Next validation
Live Unreal/Blender/render-provider tests must measure LOD transitions, material consistency, camera output and cross-provider appearance before production acceptance.
