## Integration rules

1. **Reference before import.** External repositories are indexed as dependencies/research references; source code is not copied automatically.
2. **Licence gate.** Any future source-code incorporation requires licence compatibility and dependency/security review.
3. **Algorithm lineage.** New implementations receive a Biupiu algorithm ID/version and never overwrite a released historical version.
4. **Verification separation.** AI generation and verification are separate layers. A model output is not considered physically or scientifically validated merely because an AI generated it.
5. **Physics gate.** Safety-critical or physical-world systems use constraints, simulation and validation before operational use.
6. **Blockchain minimization.** Large/private research data remains off-chain; blockchain is used for approved identifiers, hashes, provenance, permissions and transactions.
7. **Human release gate.** Autonomous agents do not receive unrestricted authority to publish IP, spend funds, deploy contracts or promote private R&D into customer-facing Biupiu World.
8. **Private R&D isolation.** Experimental algorithms and simulations may be developed in the private R&D centre before an explicit promotion decision.
9. **Digital Twin lineage.** Simulation models, geometry, algorithms, telemetry and release records remain traceable to source/version/commit where practical.
10. **Commercial boundary.** These research resources remain in the personal R&D repository unless separately approved for the commercial Biupiu registry.
