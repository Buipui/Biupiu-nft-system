# Biupiu World — Ancient Civilisations & Regional Environment Model v1.0

**Status:** World architecture / sandbox specification
**Date:** 18 September 2026

## Purpose
Biupiu World extends the Main Hub civilisation sandboxes into a navigable research, learning, simulation and innovation world. Ancient civilisations are placed inside relevant geographic/regional sandboxes and represented as separate environments rather than one blended historical world.

Previously collected Biupiu concepts, research records, computational-geometry models, farming systems, metallurgy studies, textile/material studies, marine concepts and other approved assets can be referenced through Research ID/Asset ID and evidence state.

## Navigation
MAIN HUB → REGION → CIVILISATION ENVIRONMENT → HISTORICAL ZONE → DEPARTMENT SPACE → WORKSHOP/LAB → PROJECT

## Regional civilisation map

### Southern African Region
Individually sourced communities, archaeological cultures and historical periods, with zones for Heritage & Archaeology, Ancient Agriculture & Water, Fibre/Textiles, Ceramics & Earth Materials, Metalworking where supported, Landscape/GIS reconstruction and Modern Innovation.

### North African / Mediterranean Region
Initial environments: Ancient Egypt, Nubian/Kushite research environment, and North African/Mediterranean archaeological environments. Zones: Nile Agriculture & Water, Architecture/Geometry, Textiles, Ceramics/Glass/Pigments, Metallurgy, Trade/Transport and Reconstruction Lab.

### Mesopotamian Region
Initial environments: Sumerian, Akkadian, Babylonian and Assyrian. Zones: Irrigation/Agriculture, Clay/Brick/Ceramics, Metallurgy, Textiles, Writing/Knowledge Systems, Architecture/Urban Systems and Reconstruction Lab.

### South Asian Region
Initial environments: Indus Valley/Harappan and later South Asian historical technology environments, including India regional research environments. Zones: Water/Agriculture, Cotton/Textiles, Metallurgy, Ceramics/Materials, Mathematics/Geometry, Trade Networks and Reconstruction Lab.

### East Asian Region
Initial environments: ancient Chinese cultural/technological environments and Japanese historical environments, including the previously requested feudal-Japan themed space. Zones: Rice Agriculture/Water, Silk/Textiles, Ceramics/Kilns, Metallurgy/Blades, Woodworking, Architecture, Maritime/Trade and Reconstruction Lab. The Japanese environment can include a dedicated Katana Workshop. Game/mod assets remain labelled as third-party or fictional/game-derived material and are not historical evidence.

### Ancient Americas Region
Initial environments: Mesoamerican and Andean civilisation environments plus individually sourced archaeological cultures. Zones: Agriculture/Water, Textiles/Fibres, Ceramics, Stoneworking, Architecture/Geometry, Landscape Reconstruction and Materials Lab.

## Damascus / Levant gateway
Damascus is modelled as a regional historical city/trade/metalworking environment, not as a standalone civilisation. It can contain a Damascus historical urban environment, Damascus-steel research environment, metalworking workshop, trade-route map, materials laboratory and reconstruction lab. Claims about Damascus steel remain source- and evidence-specific.

## Asset/model integration
Asset → Asset ID → Research ID → Region → Civilisation → Environment → Evidence State.

Asset classes include 3D world/architectural models, computational geometry, farming reconstructions, metallurgy/materials models, textile/fibre models, hydrofoil/marine concepts, mobility concepts, photonics/energy concepts, maps/GIS, artwork/NFT provenance assets and educational visualisations. The glass-hydrofoil concept remains in a Marine Innovation Zone/showcase rather than being presented as an ancient artefact.

## Evidence firewall
Every historical object/environment records civilisation/culture, region, site/location, date/period, source provenance, evidence classification, reconstruction status, asset provenance and licence/rights status.

Evidence states: DOCUMENTED | RECONSTRUCTED | EXPERIMENTAL | HYPOTHESIS | SPECULATIVE.

## Department firewall
The world does not override subscriber restrictions. A METALLURGY subscriber entering Mesopotamia receives the Metalworking Environment only; a TEXTILES subscriber entering South Asia receives the Textile Environment only. Multiple explicit department entitlements permit multiple department spaces.

## World-building rule
Biupiu World is a research-backed interactive sandbox, not a claim that every reconstructed building, machine, process or civilisation is historically exact. Incomplete evidence is visibly identified as reconstruction or creative interpretation.

## Development phases
W0 world registry and access model; W1 Main Hub → Region → Civilisation navigation; W2 historical environment shells and asset registry; W3 interactive scenes/maps/workshops; W4 research-object interaction and reconstruction tools; W5 evidence-constrained AI guide; W6 modern Innovation Zones connecting validated research to Biupiu R&D.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Each civilisation has its own environment ID.
2. Civilisations are placed within regional sandboxes.
3. Damascus is treated as a historical regional city/gateway.
4. Existing Biupiu assets are referenced through provenance.
5. Subscriber department controls remain active inside every world.
6. Historical reconstruction cannot silently become established evidence.
7. Game/mod/creative assets are labelled separately from historical evidence.
8. New civilisations can be added without changing the access-control architecture.
