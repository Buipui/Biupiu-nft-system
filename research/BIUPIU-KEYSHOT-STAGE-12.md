# BIUPIU KEYSHOT STAGE 12 — VISUAL ASSET MANIFEST

Stage 12 establishes the asset-level validation boundary between the R&D asset repository and visualization/render execution.

Flow:
Asset source -> geometry validation -> material provenance check -> environment selection -> visual asset manifest -> render job -> output registry.

This prevents incomplete geometry, restricted materials and missing environment definitions from entering the render queue.

Target domains include automotive, marine, eVTOL/helicopter, microturbine, biocomposites, bio-resins/adhesives, textiles, engineering and product/showreel assets.
