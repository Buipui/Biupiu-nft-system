# Biupiu MATERIALS v1.0

**Status:** Architecture specification
**Date:** 18 September 2026

## Purpose

A culturally contextualised materials research space with Southern African, West African, North African/Mediterranean, South Asian, East Asian and Ancient Americas environments, plus an Advanced Materials Laboratory. It covers documented ceramics, glass, pigments, stone, furnaces, refractories, natural binders, coatings, composites and historical material processing, alongside modern ceramics, hemp carbon, biochar, coatings, metamaterials and multifunctional-material R&D. Historical claims remain evidence-classified and modern performance claims require testing. Access is MATERIALS entitlement only.

## Environment rule

Each environment combines documented cultural context with a specialist workspace. Modern experiments are recorded separately from historical reconstruction. Cross-department resources require explicit SHARED classification.
