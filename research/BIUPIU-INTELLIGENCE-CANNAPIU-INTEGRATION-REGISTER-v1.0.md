## New division

`CANNAPIU` — modular franchise, mobile hospitality, cultivation-technology and smart-farming deployment division modelled on the Dankery Members Club architecture.

## Intelligence-layer interfaces

- `SITE_CONTEXT`: jurisdiction, site permission, weather exposure, footprint and operating constraints
- `ASSET_GRAPH`: vehicle, panels, tent, awning, machinery, sensors and service parts
- `MATERIAL_LEDGER`: resin, coating, vinyl/PPF, hemp canvas, composite and test evidence
- `STRUCTURAL_SOLVER`: load cases, equilibrium, stress, deflection, buckling and stability screening
- `SENSOR_TELEMETRY`: wind, tilt, load, temperature, humidity, rain and equipment health
- `DMS`: franchise, asset, task, maintenance, inspection, incident and training records
- `DIGITAL_TWIN`: versioned geometry, material properties, configuration and simulation outputs
- `PROVENANCE`: source, licence, test, revision, approval and responsible reviewer
- `FAILURE_LEARNING`: fault injection, observed failure, corrective action and regression test

## Product-to-customer routing

CannaPiu franchisees can become customers of multiple Biupiu divisions: franchise systems, modular vehicles, composite panels, coatings, bio-derived vinyl/PPF, hemp canvas, composite tents/awnings, machinery, sensors, robotics, DMS, software, maintenance and upgrades.

## Revenue-accounting rule

Track external sales separately from intercompany transfers. Consolidated Biupiu reporting must eliminate internal sales between subsidiaries and recognise recurring software, maintenance, licensing and franchise income according to applicable accounting rules.
