# Biupiu Virtual Laboratory v1.0

## Simulation domains
- CFD and fluid dynamics
- FEA/FEM
- thermal systems
- electromagnetics
- optical propagation / FSO
- metamaterials and metasurfaces
- agricultural water and soil models
- energy systems
- robotics and control
- manufacturing processes
- geometry/topology optimisation
- digital-twin calibration

## Validation rule
Simulation is evidence for the modelled assumptions and boundary conditions. It does not replace physical testing or independent replication.
