## Evidence discipline
Public research, vendor documentation and open-source software are inputs, not
automatically Biupiu-owned IP. Preserve source URL, retrieval date, licence,
hash/version and the exact Biupiu contribution for each invention record.

## Protection controls
- Keep invention records private until an IP professional advises on disclosure.
- Do not publish enabling implementation details before a filing decision where
  patent protection may be relevant.
- Separate third-party code/licences from Biupiu-authored code.
- Maintain signed/hashable invention snapshots and change history.
- Obtain professional South African and international patent/FTO advice before
  relying on this register as a legal protection mechanism.
