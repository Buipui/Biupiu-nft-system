# Biupiu Department Services v1

Gate 20 replaces capability-only surfaces with a service contract.

The shared runtime now defines service requests and adapters for:
- WORLD
- RESEARCH_REPOSITORY
- AUTOMATION
- AI

A service adapter reports whether a service is available and returns a structured result.

This gate establishes the integration boundary only. It does not claim that external research systems, AI providers, automation hardware, or production APIs are connected.
