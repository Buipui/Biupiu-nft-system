# Biupiu World Treasury Simulator v1.0

## Purpose
A digital-lab/workshop asset for teaching treasury management, budgeting, cash flow, allocation, reserves and economic decision-making inside a simulated Biupiu World.

The simulator uses **virtual units only**. It does not hold real money, BPU, securities or crypto assets.

## Treasury accounts
- Operating reserve
- Ecosystem development
- Research & education
- Marketplace settlement
- Creator/community pool
- Liquidity/network research reserve
- Emergency reserve

## Core loop
Revenue/activity -> treasury allocation -> approved simulated spending -> reserve changes -> reporting -> audit event.

## Controls
- double-entry-style ledger
- immutable simulation event IDs
- role permissions
- approval thresholds
- budget limits
- transaction receipts
- monthly/annual reports
- scenario reset
- full audit log

## Teaching modes
1. Beginner: income, expenses, saving and budgeting.
2. Intermediate: treasury allocation, cash flow and opportunity cost.
3. Advanced: liquidity, velocity, reserves, scenario stress tests and market structure.
4. Trading laboratory: paper trading only; no real-money execution.

## Important separation
The treasury simulator is not the production BPU treasury. A future production treasury must have separately approved legal, governance, custody, security and accounting controls.
