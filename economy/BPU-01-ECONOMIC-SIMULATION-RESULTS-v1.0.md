# BPU-01 Illustrative Economic Simulation Results v1.0

## Status

**Simulation only. Not a BPU price prediction.**

The model tests marketplace transaction economics using the illustrative allocation structure:

- Seller/creator: 70%
- Marketplace/service: 15%
- Treasury/ecosystem: 10%
- Liquidity/network: 5%

These percentages are placeholders for modelling and are **not approved production tokenomics**.

## Scenario outputs

| Scenario | Users | Purchases/user | Avg purchase | Transactions | Gross BPU settled | Treasury BPU | Liquidity/network BPU |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Pilot | 100 | 2 | 50 BPU | 200 | 10,000 BPU | 1,000 BPU | 500 BPU |
| Growth | 1,000 | 3 | 60 BPU | 3,000 | 180,000 BPU | 18,000 BPU | 9,000 BPU |
| Scale | 10,000 | 4 | 75 BPU | 40,000 | 3,000,000 BPU | 300,000 BPU | 150,000 BPU |

## Interpretation

The model demonstrates a mechanical relationship:

**Marketplace activity -> BPU settlement volume -> disclosed allocation flows.**

It does **not** demonstrate that BPU price will rise.

For example, higher transaction activity could increase utility and demand, but actual market price would depend on independent supply and demand, liquidity, market structure, regulation and other factors.
