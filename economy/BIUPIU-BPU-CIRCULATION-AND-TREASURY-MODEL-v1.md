# Biupiu World BPU Circulation & Treasury Model v1.0

## User requirement
Biupiu World should not sell a second "Biupiu World token" as a substitute for BPU. Markets use the future Biupiu Coin (BPU) as the designated settlement asset.

## Important distinction
A marketplace payment can economically support the BPU ecosystem without promising that BPU will appreciate. The system must never use artificial trading, wash trading, coordinated volume, guaranteed returns, or misleading price-support claims.

## Proposed flow

Buyer:
Fiat / approved cryptocurrency
-> approved BPU acquisition route
-> BPU wallet
-> Biupiu World purchase

Marketplace:
BPU purchase price
-> seller/creator allocation
-> marketplace/service allocation
-> disclosed treasury/ecosystem allocation
-> disclosed liquidity/market-support allocation, if legally permitted

The exact percentages are NOT fixed by this architecture. They must be established in the BPU tokenomics and legal/compliance design before launch.

## What the ecosystem allocation can fund
Subject to the final legal structure:
- blockchain/network infrastructure
- security audits
- liquidity provision
- ecosystem development
- marketplace development
- research grants
- creator/developer incentives
- operating reserves

Any liquidity or treasury activity must be transparent, governed and independently auditable.

## Economic objective
The intended flywheel is utility-led:

More useful Biupiu World products
-> more marketplace activity
-> more legitimate demand for BPU
-> greater on-chain usage
-> deeper liquidity and better price discovery
-> stronger ecosystem utility

This is an economic model, not a promise of price appreciation.

## Trading
BPU may eventually be tradable on approved external or native markets if legally and technically permitted. Trading volume must come from independent market participants and genuine transactions.

The system must explicitly prohibit:
- wash trading
- self-trading to manufacture volume
- fake orders
- coordinated price manipulation
- guaranteed-return claims
- fabricated liquidity or reserves
- misleading market statistics

## Wallet integration
The user may connect their own compatible crypto wallet when the real BPU network and wallet standards are defined. Biupiu World should use wallet-connect/account-abstraction interfaces rather than requiring private keys to be stored by the application.

## Launch gates
Before real BPU commerce:
1. Define BPU supply and issuance.
2. Define the BPU blockchain or selected network.
3. Define treasury and liquidity rules.
4. Define marketplace allocation percentages.
5. Complete legal/compliance review in relevant jurisdictions.
6. Implement audited wallet/payment contracts.
7. Test on testnet/local network.
8. Publish transparent tokenomics and reserve/treasury reporting.
9. Only then activate production settlement.

## Status
This is an architecture/specification. It does not create BPU, sell BPU, establish a live market, or guarantee any value.
