## C-04 — Real coin activation gate
Activation requires all of the following:
- published token/network specification;
- verified contract or native-chain identity;
- wallet/key-management design;
- security review and test coverage;
- testnet validation where applicable;
- explicit production configuration;
- legal/accounting review appropriate to the jurisdiction;
- user-visible confirmation that the asset is live.

Until C-04 passes, Biupiu Coin remains a future compatibility target only.
