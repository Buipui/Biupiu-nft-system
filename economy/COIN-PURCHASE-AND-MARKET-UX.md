# Biupiu Coin Purchase & Market UX v1.0

## Wallet screen
Display:
- BPU Balance
- Available
- Pending
- Reserved
- Estimated value
- Buy BPU
- Send
- Receive
- Transaction history

## Checkout
When a user lacks sufficient BPU:

NOT ENOUGH BPU
This market requires BPU. Buy BPU with supported money or cryptocurrency to continue.

Buttons:
- Buy BPU
- Add funds
- Cancel

After purchase:
BPU received -> refresh verified balance -> return to checkout

## Purchase confirmation
Show item, seller, BPU amount, network/asset, fees, total, destination, refund policy and final authorization button.

No transaction should be submitted from a passive page load or without explicit confirmation.

## Marketplace principle
BPU is the common settlement unit for BPU-required Biupiu World markets. External money/cryptocurrency is an acquisition route, not a substitute for the required BPU checkout balance unless a future market explicitly enables an alternative.
