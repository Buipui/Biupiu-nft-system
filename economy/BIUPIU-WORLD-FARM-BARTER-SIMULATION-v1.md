# Biupiu World Farm Barter Simulation v1.0

## Brand-aligned concept

Biupiu World can teach economics through **crops, farms, resources and barter** rather than making every educational experience about financial markets.

A subscriber enters a virtual regenerative farm, produces crops, manages resources and trades surplus with other farms.

## Example economy

Farm A:
- 100 units maize
- 20 units beans

Farm B:
- 30 units maize
- 80 units beans

Example barter:
**20 maize -> 10 beans**

The simulation then measures:
- comparative scarcity
- opportunity cost
- negotiation
- supply and demand
- inventory
- transport cost
- spoilage
- seasonal production
- resource constraints
- barter ratios
- money as a medium of exchange
- transition from barter to BPU-denominated commerce

## Subscriber gameplay loop

Plant -> grow -> harvest -> store -> negotiate -> barter -> reinvest -> expand.

Subscribers should receive **virtual farm assets and simulation credits**, not guaranteed financial returns.

## Advanced scenarios

- drought
- surplus harvest
- crop failure
- changing barter ratios
- cooperative farming
- shared irrigation
- transport shortages
- market shocks
- seasonal demand
- food processing
- regenerative soil improvements

## BPU bridge

BPU should only appear as a later educational layer:

Barter -> virtual currency -> BPU economic model -> real-world financial literacy.

The simulation must clearly distinguish virtual units from real BPU.

## Learning objective

The goal is to let users discover economic principles by operating a small virtual agricultural economy and observing the consequences of their choices.
