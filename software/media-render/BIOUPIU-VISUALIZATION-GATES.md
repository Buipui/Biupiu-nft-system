# Biupiu Visualization Gates

## Gate V0 — Toolchain architecture
Defined in this package.

## Gate V1 — Master asset
A Blender/CAD/USD master asset exists and is versioned.

## Gate V2 — Material/lighting validation
Materials, scale, lighting and camera conventions are documented.

## Gate V3 — Professional still render
Approved still render generated with provenance metadata.

## Gate V4 — Motion
Turntable, animation or walkthrough generated from the controlled master asset.

## Gate V5 — Cinematic
AI-assisted or traditional video assembled without presenting generated visuals as engineering proof.

## Gate V6 — Digital twin
Interactive Unreal/Twinmotion representation linked to the relevant research object.

## Gate V7 — Archive
Final media, source version, toolchain, settings and provenance are archived in the R&D OS.
