# Biupiu AI Mathematical Integration Contract v1.0

AI pipeline:
AI PROPOSAL -> MATH NORMALIZATION -> SOLVER(S) -> VERIFICATION -> DIGITAL TWIN -> ENGINEERING REVIEW

AI may propose equations, decompositions, solver choices, geometric constructions, optimisation objectives and surrogate models. Mathematical verification determines validity.

Evidence labels:
AI_PROPOSAL
DERIVED_RESULT
FORMALLY_VERIFIED
NUMERICALLY_VALIDATED
SIMULATION_RESULT
EXPERIMENTAL_MEASUREMENT

AlphaGeometry2 is an optional external geometry-proof adapter. OpenProver and QED are research candidates for formal/multi-agent proof experiments. None is silently copied into the core.
