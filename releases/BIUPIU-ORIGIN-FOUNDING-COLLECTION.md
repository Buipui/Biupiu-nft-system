# BIUPIU / ORIGIN — Founding Collection v1.0

**Purpose:** Initial NFT crowdfunding release to help establish the Biupiu research, computational-art and digital archive ecosystem.

## Collection Positioning

BIUPIU / ORIGIN is the founding collector-facing collection. It documents the beginning of the Biupiu ecosystem through original digital and computational artwork connected to research themes.

## Ancient Systems × Computational Geometry Series

The first specialist series can develop from:

- WATER — ancient hydraulic systems and computational flow geometry
- EARTH — terraces, agriculture and landscape geometry
- STONE — architecture and megalithic geometry
- METAL — metallurgy and mineral structures
- SKY — astronomical orientation
- NETWORK — settlements, trade and transport
- LIFE — biological geometry and traditional ecological systems
- TRANSFORMATION — futuristic computational reinterpretations

## Release Classes

**Class A — Art:** primarily aesthetic digital/generative works.

**Class B — Research Art:** artwork linked to documented Biupiu research.

**Class C — Computational Artifact:** artwork linked to an algorithm, parameters and reproducible generation record where appropriate.

## Collector Information

Each release should disclose its Research ID, NFT ID, evidence classification, edition size, artwork description, provenance, applicable licence and crowdfunding purpose.

NFT ownership does not automatically transfer Biupiu patents, inventions, trademarks, proprietary research, confidential information, biological materials, source code or underlying scientific discoveries.

## Funding Intent

Funds may support research and development, computation, software, experimental development, agricultural/materials research, prototyping, digital infrastructure, documentation, artwork generation, archive/website development and IP development.

Specific allocation percentages and financial targets should be added only after the crowdfunding budget and legal/financial review are completed.

## Launch Gate

No NFT should be listed for sale until:

- artwork originality is documented;
- third-party rights/licences are checked;
- research sources are recorded;
- evidence classification is assigned;
- IP/patent sensitivity is reviewed;
- metadata is reviewed;
- NFT licence is approved;
- crowdfunding language is reviewed;
- final artwork and metadata hashes are recorded where applicable.
