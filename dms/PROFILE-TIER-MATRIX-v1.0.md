# Biupiu DMS Profile & Subscription Tier Matrix v1.0

## Identity profiles

ROOT — full administrative authority.
PLATFORM_ADMIN — platform operations within assigned permissions.
DEPARTMENT_ADMIN — assigned department administration.
SITE_MANAGER — assigned site operations.
STAFF_OPERATOR — task-level operations.
R&D_USER — authorized R&D projects.
CUSTOMER — customer account/product access.
CUSTOMER_R&D — customer plus explicit research participation.
DEVICE_SERVICE — machine identity.

## Commercial tiers

CORE — essential product functions.
STANDARD — monitoring and standard digital features.
PRO — advanced analytics, diagnostics and automation where supported.
ENTERPRISE — multi-site DMS, APIs, administration and advanced analytics.
R&D PARTNER — opt-in experimental/research features.

## Effective-access rule

Effective permissions = Role permissions ∩ Organization/Site scope ∩ Product capability ∩ Subscription entitlements ∩ Policy state.

An upgrade can add eligible optional functions after MIC refresh. A downgrade removes optional entitlements after the effective date/grace period. Historical records remain retained according to policy.

Role permissions and subscription entitlements are separate: paying for a tier does not grant administrative privileges.
